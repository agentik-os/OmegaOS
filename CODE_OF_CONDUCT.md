# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want OmegaOS to be a project people are glad they contributed to, regardless of experience, background, or identity. Everyone interacting in the repo, issues, and pull requests is expected to act with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be civil and assume good faith. Critique the code, not the person.
- Welcome questions, including basic ones.
- Accept that decisions are made with reasons, and disagree with arguments rather than hostility.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

If someone is breaking this, report it privately: open a report under the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**), which reaches the maintainers in a private thread. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, remove comments, or ban a contributor whose behavior is harmful.

This is a short, plain-language code of conduct in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org). If something here is unclear, the Covenant's full text is a reasonable tie-breaker.
