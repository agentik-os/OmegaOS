# Protocol: Reset, 5 Minutes

The spiral interrupt. Run it before an interaction when the user is winding up, and after one when they cannot put it down. It is not a calming exercise and it does not try to remove the anxiety. It restores availability: the ability to be in the room with another person rather than inside a commentary about oneself.

Anxious and present beats calm and absent. The reset targets presence, not the feeling.

## Steps
1. **Minute 1, the body.** Six breaths with the exhale longer than the inhale. Feet felt on the floor, weight settled into them. Shoulders down and back, jaw and tongue loose, hands unclenched. Physiology first, because a nervous system mid-spike does not respond to argument.
2. **Minute 2, name it accurately.** One sentence, out loud or written: "I am nervous, my chest is tight, my thoughts are fast." Naming the state rather than the story. Anxiety and excitement share most of their physiology, and the label attached to the arousal changes what the user does with it **[E2]**. Do not claim more for that than it earns: this is relabelling, not elimination.
3. **Minute 3, strip the story to facts.** What is actually happening, with the interpretation removed. Before: "I am going to a room with people in it." After: "I asked, they said no." What is left when the adjectives go is almost always survivable and almost always small.
4. **Minute 4, restore the outward turn.** The spiral is attention pointed inward at a running self-evaluation. Point it out: name three specific things in the room that have nothing to do with the user. This is the same muscle as SEE in first_conversation.md, and practising it here is why it is available later.
5. **Minute 5, one action.** Before an interaction: the smallest next physical move (walk in, order something, put the phone away, say one word to one person). After an interaction: close it out loud, then a real action that engages the body and is not thinking. Both cases end in a movement, never in a resolution.

## The 90-second field version
For use standing up, in the moment, with people around.
1. One long exhale, twice as long as the inhale.
2. Feet, shoulders, jaw.
3. Three things in the room, out loud in the head, none of them about the user.
4. One physical move.

## Which spiral is this
| Timing | What it sounds like | The specific correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before | "This is going to be humiliating" | It is a prediction, not information. Shrink the next move until it needs no courage, then make that move |
| During, in a gap | "They think I am boring" | Attention has turned inward. Step 4, immediately, then ask them a real question |
| After, same day | Replaying the transcript for the fatal line | There is rarely a fatal line. One debrief, inside the bound, then closed |
| After, days later | Still returning to it | The bound has been passed. This is rumination. Close it and do something physical, and if it will not close, see the stop rules |

## Stop rules
- The reset is not a debrief. Do not analyse the interaction inside it, do not extract lessons, do not score anything. Analysis while flooded produces a global verdict about the self and installs it. Regulate first, analyse later or not at all.
- Do not use the reset to talk the user out of the feeling. The feeling is allowed and it is not the problem.
- Do not run it more than twice for the same event. A third run means it is not working, and the answer is a different tool rather than another repetition.
- No alcohol as the reset. If it is being used that way, that is the finding, and it routes out.
- Route to a qualified professional under **C** if the reset is needed daily, if panic responses are present, if the spiral involves self-harm, or if the user cannot stop returning to a specific person. Persistent rumination and compulsive pursuit are not managed by a breathing exercise, and offering one as if it were is the failure.

## Required closure
- Decision or output: the state named, and one physical action taken.
- Owner: the user, alone, in about five minutes.
- Observable completion evidence: the action happened. A reset that ends in a plan rather than a movement did not complete.
- Review trigger: none. This protocol closes on the action. If it is being run repeatedly for the same event, that itself routes to post_rejection_debrief.md or out under **C**.
- Memory and handoff instruction: log nothing by default. This is a moment, not a record. Hand a pre-interaction spiral to approach_anxiety_ladder.md (the dose is too high), a post-interaction spiral to interaction_debrief.md once inside the bound, and a persistent one to a qualified professional.
