# Phase 01 — Readiness and Fit

> *The honest gate before any commitment is made.*

---

## Purpose

Before a euro is spent or a contract signed, we run a structured qualification to
determine whether the CAIO On Demand engagement is the right fit for your company
right now. The verdict may be Go, Not yet, or Redirect — and all three are useful
outcomes. A Not-yet result includes a concrete plan to close the gap; a Redirect
names the specific alternative that better fits your current situation.

---

## What happens

1. **Intake.** You or a colleague fills in the basics: company size, industry,
   current tool stack, regulatory context, and the one trigger that made you reach
   out. We also take a quick look at your public website to come in informed.

2. **The qualification call (30 minutes).** We run a structured diagnostic across
   nine dimensions of AI readiness: executive sponsorship, tooling and API exposure,
   strategy and use-case clarity, data readiness, governance and compliance, culture
   and change appetite, AI literacy, infrastructure, and budget commitment. Each
   dimension is scored 0 to 4 with evidence from the call.

3. **Readiness Index and tier.** Scores are weighted into a Readiness Index (0–100)
   and a maturity tier: Nascent, Emerging, Ready, or Leading.

4. **Verdict and hard gates.** Five hard gates must pass regardless of the overall
   index: a named budget-holding sponsor, core tools that expose usable APIs, at
   least one concrete use case, no unresolved compliance blocker, and a genuine
   readiness to start. Any gate that fails determines the outcome.

5. **Engagement shape.** On a Go, we specify which phases apply, an indicative
   duration (1 to 3 months), the number of members in scope, and an indicative
   investment figure calculated from the published grid.

6. **Handoff or redirect.** A Go moves to contract (Statement of Work) then directly
   into Phase 02 — Discovery. A Not-yet receives the Gap-to-Target plan and a
   re-qualify date. A Redirect receives a named recommendation of what to do instead.

---

## What you receive

Deliverables in this phase's `templates/` folder:

- **`AI-Readiness-Scorecard.md`** — nine dimensions each scored 0–4 with the cited
  evidence from your call and site scan; Readiness Index and maturity tier.
- **`Go-No-Go-Brief.md`** — a one-page executive summary of the verdict, the honest
  reasoning behind it, and the immediate next step.
- **`Recommended-Engagement.md`** — on a Go: the engagement shape (phases, duration,
  members, indicative investment); on a Not-yet: the path back; on a Redirect: the
  named alternative.
- **`Gap-To-Target-Plan.md`** — the specific items to resolve before or early in the
  engagement to de-risk it, or to clear a Not-yet verdict.

---

## What we need from you

- One named participant with budget authority and decision power (not a delegate)
- 30 minutes of genuine availability for the qualification call
- Your company website URL
- An honest account of what triggered your interest (the "why now")
- Your current tool stack, even if approximate
- Any known regulatory constraints (GDPR, SOC2, HIPAA, sector-specific)

---

## Duration

A single 30-minute call. Deliverables are typically produced within 24 hours.

---

## How you will know this phase is complete

You hold a written verdict — Go, Not-yet, or Redirect — with the reasoning clearly
stated and a named next step. On a Go, a draft Statement of Work is handed to you
within 48 hours. On a Not-yet, the Gap-to-Target plan tells you exactly what to fix
and when to come back. On a Redirect, you have a specific recommendation to act on.

There is no ambiguity at the end of this phase: the verdict is one word and the
reasoning fits on one page.
