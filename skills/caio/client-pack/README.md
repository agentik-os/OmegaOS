# CAIO On Demand — Client Pack

This pack is handed to a prospective or active client at the start of the CAIO On
Demand engagement. It explains every phase, every deliverable, and every request
we make — so no step of the accompaniment is a surprise.

## How to use this pack

**Start with the Inner Guide.** `INNER-GUIDE.md` (or the PDF `00-INNER-GUIDE.pdf`)
walks the whole accompaniment from first call to autonomous system. It covers the
three principles, the investment model, the internal-mastery promise, and a "how to
prepare" section you can act on before your first meeting.

**Then go to the phase you are in.** Each numbered folder holds a `PLAYBOOK.md`
that gives the concrete picture for that phase: what happens step by step, exactly
what you receive (named deliverable files), what we need from you, the expected
duration, and a concrete signal that tells you the phase is complete.

**The templates/ subfolders** hold the fillable deliverable templates that ship with
each phase. Your assigned CAIO operator fills these during the engagement; you can
inspect them at any time.
