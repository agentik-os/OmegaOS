# Persona Language Packs

How to ask the **same 12 chapters** in each job family's native language. Load only the section that matches the person. The intent is constant; only the wording, the example tools, and the framing change.

**Universal rule:** translate the three "scary" chapters carefully for everyone —
- **Ch.5 Connections/integrations** → for non-tech: *"Do your tools talk to each other, or do you move things by hand between them?"*
- **Ch.6 AI & automation** → for non-tech: *"What happens by itself today, and what do you still do manually?"* (avoid "automation stack", "agents", "LLM").
- **Ch.7 Frictions** → for everyone: *"What's the part of your week you wish would just disappear?"*

Each pack lists: **Voice** · **What they care about** · **Tools they may name** · **Phrasing tips** · **Avoid**.

---

## EXEC — Executive / C-suite (CEO, COO, GM, Founder)
- **Voice:** strategic, outcome-first, time-scarce. Talk in decisions, leverage, bottlenecks, where the company loses money or speed.
- **Care about:** what only they can decide, where the org is blocked, visibility into the business, what eats their week that shouldn't.
- **Tools they may name:** board decks, dashboards/BI, email, calendar, Slack/Teams, a CRM summary, finance reports, Notion/docs.
- **Phrasing tips:** "Where does the company slow down and wait on you?" · "What report or number do you chase every week by hand?" · "If you cloned yourself, what would the clone do all day?"
- **Avoid:** implementation detail, vendor names, anything that sounds like a tech demo. They want leverage, not features.

## SALES — Sales / Revenue (CRO, Head of Sales, AE, SDR lead)
- **Voice:** pipeline, deals, quota, follow-ups, speed-to-lead, win/loss.
- **Care about:** more time selling vs. admin, faster follow-up, clean pipeline, not dropping leads.
- **Tools they may name:** CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive), email/sequencer, LinkedIn, dialer, calendar, proposal/CPQ, WhatsApp.
- **Phrasing tips:** "Walk me through a deal from first contact to closed." · "What do you re-type into the CRM after a call?" · "Where do leads go cold while you're busy?"
- **Avoid:** "data pipeline", "enrichment API". Say "where the lead info comes from and where it has to go".

## MARKETING — Marketing / Growth (CMO, Brand, Content, Social, Comms)
- **Voice:** campaigns, content, channels, brand, funnel, reporting.
- **Care about:** producing more without losing quality/brand, faster reporting, repurposing, consistency.
- **Tools they may name:** CMS, social schedulers, Canva/Figma, analytics (GA, Meta), email platform, ad managers, Notion/Asana.
- **Phrasing tips:** "Take me through making one campaign end to end." · "What do you rebuild from scratch every time that you wish you could reuse?" · "Which numbers do you stitch together by hand for a report?"
- **Avoid:** "GenAI orchestration", "RAG". Say "tools that help you draft or repurpose content".

## FINANCE — Finance / Accounting (CFO, Controller, FP&A, Accountant)
- **Voice:** precise, control-minded, period-based (close, month-end, quarter). Accuracy and audit trail matter.
- **Care about:** fewer manual reconciliations, faster close, fewer spreadsheet errors, clean numbers.
- **Tools they may name:** ERP (SAP, NetSuite, Sage), accounting (QuickBooks, Xero), Excel/Sheets, banking portals, expense tools, BI.
- **Phrasing tips:** "Walk me through your month-end close." · "What do you copy between the bank, the ERP, and your spreadsheets?" · "Where do errors creep in, and what do you double-check by hand?"
- **Avoid:** "agentic workflow". Say "steps that could run on their own with you approving". Stress that nothing financial gets decided without a human — they'll relax.

## OPS — Operations (Ops Manager, Logistics, Supply, PMO)
- **Voice:** process, throughput, handoffs, SLAs, exceptions.
- **Care about:** smoother handoffs, fewer dropped balls, status visibility, less chasing people.
- **Tools they may name:** project tools (Asana, Monday, Jira), spreadsheets, ERP/inventory, ticketing, Slack/Teams, forms.
- **Phrasing tips:** "Trace one order/request from start to finish — who touches it?" · "Where does work sit waiting?" · "What status update do you assemble by hand?"
- **Avoid:** "event-driven architecture". Say "what triggers the next step, and is it automatic or manual?".

## HR — People / HR / Talent (CHRO, HRBP, Recruiter)
- **Voice:** people-first, lifecycle (hire → onboard → develop → exit), confidential, compliant.
- **Care about:** less admin, faster hiring, smoother onboarding, keeping the human touch.
- **Tools they may name:** ATS, HRIS (BambooHR, Workday), payroll, LinkedIn, email, e-sign, surveys.
- **Phrasing tips:** "Walk me through hiring one person, offer to first day." · "What forms or messages do you send over and over?" · "What part must stay human no matter what?"
- **Avoid:** anything that implies automating people decisions. Frame AI as removing paperwork, never as judging humans.

## PRODUCT — Product / Design (CPO, PM, Product Owner, Designer)
- **Voice:** users, roadmap, discovery, specs, iteration.
- **Care about:** more time on thinking/design vs. busywork, synthesizing feedback, keeping docs current.
- **Tools they may name:** Jira/Linear, Figma, Notion/Confluence, analytics, user-research tools, Slack.
- **Phrasing tips:** "How does an idea become a shipped feature?" · "What do you summarize or copy between tools?" · "Where does feedback pile up unread?"
- **Avoid:** they may be semi-technical — match their level. Light jargon OK if they use it first.

## TECH — Engineering / Tech / Data (CTO, Eng Lead, Developer, DevOps, Data, ML)
- **Voice:** speak tech freely. Systems, repos, CI/CD, APIs, data flows, services, tickets.
- **Care about:** removing toil, automating glue work, observability, not being the human cron job.
- **Tools they may name:** GitHub/GitLab, CI/CD, cloud, IaC, DBs, monitoring, Jira/Linear, Slack, internal scripts, n8n, MCP, LLM APIs.
- **Phrasing tips:** "Where are you the manual step between two systems?" · "What scripts/glue hold things together?" · "Which integrations are missing or brittle?" · "Where's the toil?"
- **Avoid:** nothing — but still anchor in *real work*, not tech for its own sake.

## CS — Customer Success / Support (Head of CS, Support Lead, Account Manager)
- **Voice:** customers, tickets, renewals, response time, satisfaction.
- **Care about:** faster answers, less repetitive replying, no customer slipping through, renewals visibility.
- **Tools they may name:** helpdesk (Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk), CRM, knowledge base, email, Slack, calendar.
- **Phrasing tips:** "Walk me through handling a typical ticket." · "Which answers do you type again and again?" · "How do you know a customer is at risk — do you spot it by hand?"
- **Avoid:** "deflection rate via RAG". Say "an assistant that drafts answers from your help docs".

## LEGAL — Legal / Compliance / Risk (GC, Counsel, Compliance, DPO)
- **Voice:** precise, risk-aware, confidentiality-first. They will worry about AI — acknowledge it.
- **Care about:** less manual review, finding clauses fast, tracking obligations, never losing control or audit trail.
- **Tools they may name:** contract/CLM tools, e-sign, document stores, email, spreadsheets, research databases.
- **Phrasing tips:** "Walk me through reviewing one contract." · "What do you search for by hand across documents?" · "What must always have a human signature and a human reviewer?"
- **Avoid:** implying autonomous legal decisions. Frame AI strictly as drafting/finding/flagging with them in control.

## GENERALIST — Admin / EA / Office Manager / "a bit of everything"
- **Voice:** plain, warm, concrete, zero jargon. They wear many hats.
- **Care about:** less repetitive admin, fewer things falling through the cracks, more time for the human parts.
- **Tools they may name:** email, calendar, spreadsheets, WhatsApp, a couple of apps, paper/notes.
- **Phrasing tips:** "Tell me about a normal day from when you sit down." · "What do you do over and over that feels like a robot could do it?" · "What do you copy from one place to another?"
- **Avoid:** ALL technical terms. If you wouldn't say it to a friend at dinner, don't say it here.

## Fast translation cheat-sheet (jargon → human)

| Don't say | Say instead |
|---|---|
| integration / API | "do these two tools talk to each other?" |
| automation / automate | "happens by itself / without you doing it" |
| workflow | "the steps you go through to get X done" |
| agent / agentic | "an assistant that can do a few steps for you, with you approving" |
| LLM / GenAI | "an AI tool that can write, summarize, or answer" |
| data pipeline | "where the information comes from and where it ends up" |
| manual process | "the part you do by hand" |
| system of record | "the main place this info officially lives" |

Use the human column with everyone except `TECH` (and `PRODUCT`/`ENG`-leaning people who use the jargon themselves first).
