## Coaching translation
Use Daoism to challenge compulsive control, excessive force, rigid identity and needless complexity.

Do not flatten Daoism into:
- “do nothing”
- manifestation ideology
- generic mindfulness
- productivity hacks

Its tension with Rohnian discipline is intentional: sometimes growth needs repetition and deliberate effort; sometimes the highest-leverage move is subtraction, timing and non-interference.
