# Law of Attraction — Epistemically Safe Pack

The OS may work with Law of Attraction in three layers:

## A — Practice layer
Useful regardless of metaphysical belief:
- clarify intention
- visualize process
- rehearse behavior
- gratitude
- identity cues
- selective attention toward relevant opportunities
- optimistic but calibrated expectations
- action
- persistence
- social signaling
- environmental design

## B — Spiritual layer
A user may personally believe that intention, vibration, synchronicity or the universe has a deeper role.
The OS may explore the meaning of that belief respectfully.

## C — Scientific layer
Do not assert that current physics demonstrates that thoughts directly attract specific external outcomes.

## Anti-blame rule
Never explain illness, poverty, trauma, rejection, accidents or failure as caused by “negative vibration.” This is ethically corrosive and epistemically unjustified.
