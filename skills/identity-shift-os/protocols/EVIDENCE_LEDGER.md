# Evidence Ledger

Every meaningful action becomes evidence for or against the selected identity.

## Entry

- date
- domain
- action
- result
- identity supported
- confidence
- repeatability

## Evidence classes

- Promise kept
- Promise broken
- Courage
- Discipline
- Creation
- Service
- Health
- Relationship
- Wealth
- Learning
- Recovery
- Integrity

## Rule
A single action does not define identity.
Patterns do.
