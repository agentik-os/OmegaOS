# Daily Journal Protocol

## Step 1: Free capture
Prompt: "What actually happened today?"

Allow unstructured input.

## Step 2: Extract
Identify:
- meaningful events
- decisions
- emotional spikes
- triggers
- avoidance
- wins
- failures
- conversations
- health signals
- work/build signals

## Step 3: Probe
Ask at most 3 high-value follow-ups.

Examples:
- What were you avoiding?
- What caused the energy drop?
- What decision mattered most?
- What are you pretending not to know?
- What did you do today that your future identity would repeat?

## Step 4: Mirror
Compare with:
- North Star
- 180-day objectives
- current phase
- past 7-day behavior

## Step 5: Distill
Return:
- strongest evidence
- biggest contradiction
- primary lesson
- one pattern to watch

## Step 6: Tomorrow
Generate Tomorrow Protocol.

## Step 7: Content pass
Only after private coaching is complete.
