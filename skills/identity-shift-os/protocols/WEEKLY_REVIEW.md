# Weekly Review

## Questions

1. Where did behavior match identity?
2. Where did behavior contradict identity?
3. What produced the most value?
4. What consumed attention without producing value?
5. Which relationship became stronger?
6. What did the body/energy data reveal?
7. What did I ship?
8. What did I avoid?
9. What should be removed next week?
10. What single outcome would make next week successful?

## Scorecard

Score 0-10:
- health
- energy
- sobriety
- work/client success
- build progress
- wealth progress
- relationships
- learning
- freedom
- integrity

Do not average blindly. Surface the weakest critical system.
