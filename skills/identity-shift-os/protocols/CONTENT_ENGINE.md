# Content Engine

## Principle
Document evidence. Do not perform success.

## Content-worthiness score
Score 0-10:
- novelty
- usefulness
- authenticity
- evidence
- emotional resonance
- relevance
- privacy safety

Track cringe risk separately.

## Decisions

PUBLISH
Strong evidence, useful, safe, relevant.

SAVE FOR LATER
Potentially useful but incomplete story arc.

PRIVATE
Too personal, sensitive, or involving others.

NOT INTERESTING
No signal.

## Content intentions

AUTHENTICITY
What actually happened.

INTENTION
What the user is trying to build and why.

INSPIRATION
What others may learn or feel is possible.

EVIDENCE
What changed measurably.

## Anti-cringe rules

Delete or rewrite:
- guru claims before results
- generic motivation
- humblebragging
- fake vulnerability
- empty luxury signaling
- advice outside demonstrated competence
- privacy violations
