## 1. Program doctrine

This program converts Rohn's philosophy into a bounded 90-day experiment. It does not attempt to install every practice simultaneously.

Keep one external result and three active disciplines:

```text
ONE 90-DAY RESULT
+ ONE PHILOSOPHY DISCIPLINE
+ ONE VALUE/WEALTH DISCIPLINE
+ ONE HEALTH/RELATIONSHIP/SPIRITUAL DISCIPLINE
```

Everything else remains in `Not Now`.

## 2. Transformation contract

Define:

- current truth and season;
- exact 90-day result;
- why, beneficiaries, and non-sacrifices;
- capability/person required;
- philosophy rule to revise;
- three disciplines;
- repeated error to interrupt;
- lead, lag, cost, and integrity indicators;
- support and environment;
- stop/pivot criteria;
- review dates.

For every baseline field, use one of `known`, `estimated`, `unknown`, or `provisional`. Never invent a score, financial fact, market proof, diagnosis, or capability. Unknowns become evidence-gathering actions during Week 0.

## 3. Daily operating floor

### Morning — 5 to 10 minutes

1. Read one Philosophy Constitution principle.
2. Review the written 90-day goal.
3. Name the attitude compatible with current facts.
4. Choose the decisive value-producing activity.
5. Rehearse the main obstacle and if-then response.

### Workday

- begin the decisive action before open-ended reaction where possible;
- record new ideas in `Not Now`;
- perform the value/marketplace discipline;
- apply one learning insight to live work;
- take the health/relationship/spiritual floor.

### Evening — 5 to 10 minutes

- Philosophy: What rule drove me?
- Attitude: What feeling influenced my choices?
- Activity: What did I actually complete?
- Results: What changed or what feedback arrived?
- Lifestyle: Did I live, relate, rest, serve, or appreciate?
- Journal: What lesson, decision, error, or gratitude deserves capture?

## 4. Weekly sequence

### Week 0 — Baseline and safety

- complete 12-domain and Five Major Pieces audits;
- define state: red/amber/green/blue;
- mark each baseline item known, estimated, unknown, or provisional and create an evidence task for material unknowns;
- establish health, money, and relationship guardrails;
- choose a provisional 90-day result and remove competing commitments;
- create workspace and review calendar.

### Week 1 — Personal philosophy inventory

- capture 25 current rules about self, time, work, money, relationships, health, faith, and failure;
- identify three repeated results;
- trace each result to activity and possible philosophy;
- select one rule to test.

### Week 2 — Philosophy Constitution v0.1

- write preamble and 12 operating laws;
- define truth, work, wealth, health, relationship, and spiritual commitments;
- create an input audit;
- begin the daily philosophy floor.

### Week 3 — Written goals ledger

- generate becoming/learning/creating/giving/owning/experiencing goals;
- assign 1/3/5/10-year horizons;
- confirm or revise the provisional 90-day outcome using Weeks 0–2 evidence;
- write becoming contract and Not Now list.

### Week 4 — Attitude and emotional agency

- map relationship to past, future, other people, self, and circumstances;
- identify one forced-positive or catastrophizing pattern;
- practice grounded hope and emotional regulation;
- write obstacle/if-then plans.

### Week 5 — Simple disciplines

- select at most three daily/weekly disciplines;
- design floor/standard/deep versions;
- attach stable cues and environment;
- list repeated errors and interruption rules.

### Week 6 — Self-education curriculum

- choose the primary capability tied to the 90-day result;
- choose source, practice, feedback, and weekly output;
- eliminate unrelated learning for the cycle;
- use the journal to preserve applied lessons.

### Week 7 — Marketplace value

- define valuable problem, customer, capability, proof, and distribution;
- complete five real market/customer conversations or equivalent evidence appropriate to context;
- identify the value bottleneck;
- choose one capability or offer improvement.

### Week 8 — Enterprise and ownership

- map time-bound income versus owned/scalable value;
- choose one small asset to build: system, case study, IP, software, media, audience, process, or equity path;
- set quality, risk, and completion criteria;
- avoid activating a second asset.

### Week 9 — Financial philosophy

- face income, expenses, taxes, debt, reserves, assets, liabilities, and runway;
- draft allocation order and lifestyle rule;
- identify professional financial/tax/legal questions;
- define waiting periods and downside controls.

### Week 10 — Associations and inputs

- audit frequent people and digital voices;
- deepen two high-quality relationships;
- set one needed boundary;
- diversify the circle rather than discarding people by status;
- revise mental input portfolio.

### Week 11 — Seasons and resilience

- label the season of each major domain;
- identify what to plant, protect, harvest, conserve, or grieve;
- correct the 90-day plan for real conditions;
- create winter and high-opportunity protocols.

### Week 12 — Lifestyle and integration

- design Lifestyle Now and Lifestyle Later;
- schedule culture, beauty, faith, friendship, rest, generosity, and celebration;
- measure the 90-day result and costs;
- update Philosophy Constitution to v0.2;
- continue, pivot, stop, or scale deliberately.

## 5. Five-pieces scorecard

Score 0–10 with evidence:

```json
{
  "philosophy": 0,
  "attitude": 0,
  "activity": 0,
  "results": 0,
  "lifestyle": 0,
  "written_goals": 0,
  "self_education": 0,
  "journal": 0,
  "associations_inputs": 0,
  "marketplace_value": 0,
  "financial_philosophy": 0,
  "season_alignment": 0
}
```

Use `null` or `unknown` instead of a number when evidence is insufficient. Do not convert an unknown into zero.

Every score needs:

- recent evidence;
- confidence;
- implication;
- one next action.

Do not average the score into human worth. Use the lowest high-leverage dimension as a possible bottleneck.

## 6. Graduation and reset

Graduate when:

- the 90-day result is completed, explicitly stopped, or responsibly pivoted;
- the philosophy rule has been tested with evidence;
- at least one valuable capability or asset improved;
- three disciplines survived normal disruption;
- the user can run the review without coach dependence;
- health, relationships, integrity, and cash risks were protected;
- a Lifestyle Now practice exists.

After a lapse:

1. restore safety and basics;
2. name the repeated error without global self-judgment;
3. run `TRACE` on trigger/rule/action/consequence/edit;
4. perform the floor at the next safe cue;
5. change one environment condition;
6. record repair in the journal.

## 7. Founder/ADHD adaptation

For idea-rich, attention-variable founders:

- the written goals ledger is not the active task list;
- one project is `Now`, one is `Next`, all others are `Not Now`;
- journal capture never activates a project;
- self-education must support the live bottleneck;
- association and input audits include tool/news/model novelty;
- weekly lifestyle prevents the system becoming a work obsession;
- high-energy days finish, prepare, connect, or recover; they do not create five commitments;
- every week ends with one visible result and one explicitly closed loop.
