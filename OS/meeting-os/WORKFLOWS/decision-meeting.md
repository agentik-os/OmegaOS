# Workflow: the decision meeting

Produces decisions with named deciders, a written record, and action items that
each have one owner and one date.

## Trigger

A meeting has passed triage and is scheduled.

## Inputs

- The decisions to be made, one per agenda item.
- The decider for each.
- The facts, options and numbers each decision needs, from Project {OS}, KPI &
  Analytics {OS}, Client {OS} or Decision {OS}.
- Prior decisions on the same topics.

## Steps

1. **Write the agenda.** Each item: the decision, the decider, the time box.
   Items with no decision become pre-read or are removed.
2. **Assemble the pre-read** and circulate it far enough ahead that it can
   actually be read. Include prior decisions on the same topics.
3. **Open the room by stating the decisions.** Not by going around the table.
4. **Run each item to its time box.** At the end of the box the item is decided,
   or parked with the condition that would unblock it and the person who owns
   that condition.
5. **Record the decision as it is taken**, including the rationale and the
   alternatives rejected. Written in the room, not reconstructed later.
6. **Convert every commitment into an action item** with exactly one owner, one
   date and one observable result. Say aloud the ones that fail this test, and
   do not record them.
7. **Do not assign to the absent.** An action for someone not present is a
   proposal until they accept it, and is recorded as such.
8. **Close the room by reading back** the decisions and the action items. This
   takes two minutes and removes almost all later disagreement about what
   happened.
9. **Distribute within the day.** Decision record to Documentation {OS}, actions
   to Execution {OS} and Team & Delegation {OS}, plan effects to Project {OS},
   boundary-crossing decisions to Review & Governance {OS}.

## Completion test

- Every agenda item is decided or parked with an unblock condition and an owner.
- Every decision record names the decision, the decider, the rationale and the
  rejected alternatives.
- Every action item has exactly one owner, one date and one observable result.
- No action is assigned to a person who was not in the room without their
  acceptance.
- The record was distributed the same day.

## Failure paths

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| discussion overruns the time box | stop, park with the unblock condition, and move on; the box is the mechanism |
| the room cannot decide | park it, name what information is missing and who will get it, and set the date it returns |
| the decider defers to consensus | record the decider anyway; someone must own it afterwards |
| new items arrive during the meeting | capture them, do not decide them, and triage them afterwards |
| a settled decision is reopened without new information | read the prior record aloud and require the new information before continuing |
