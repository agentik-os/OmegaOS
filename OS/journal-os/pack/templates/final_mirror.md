# FINAL MIRROR : DAY [N] ([YYYY-MM-DD])

<!--
Four lines. This is how every session ends, including a quick one.
Read them out, then stop talking. No summary after, no encouragement after,
no reframe after. The four lines are the close.
-->

**TODAY'S TRUTH:** [the one thing that is true about today, said plainly, in either direction]

**TODAY'S PROOF:** [the evidence behind that truth: a fact, a number, a time, a thing that shipped or did not]

**TOMORROW'S PRIORITY:** [the single thing that matters most tomorrow, drawn from the missions and no larger than one of them]

**IDENTITY VOTE:** Today I voted for the identity of someone who [behavior] `[F3]`

---

<!--
RULES

TODAY'S TRUTH
- One sentence. It may be flat: "Today was ordinary and nothing moved."
- It is not a lesson, not a moral, not a summary of the day.
- It is allowed to be uncomfortable. It is not allowed to be cruel: it names
  what happened, never what the user is.

TODAY'S PROOF
- A fact from the entry. If the truth cannot be proven from the day's facts,
  the truth is wrong and gets rewritten, not decorated.
- "Shipped the auth fix at 09:30" is proof. "I felt more focused" is not.

TOMORROW'S PRIORITY
- Exactly one, taken from the Tomorrow Protocol missions.
- On a rest day: "Tomorrow's priority is recovery. Non-negotiables stand."

IDENTITY VOTE
- Completes: "Today I voted for the identity of someone who ___".
- A behavior, never a trait. "Someone who starts before checking anything",
  not "someone who is disciplined".
- Bound to at least one fact id from the entry.
- It may be a vote in the wrong direction, and on some days it must be:
  "someone who lets the day choose what gets done" is a legitimate, honest
  vote. A day of old-self evidence closed with a flattering vote is a
  corrupted record, and the corruption compounds across a month.
- One vote for the day. Not one per domain.

EMPTY FORMS
- Truth with nothing in the day: "Today was ordinary. Nothing moved and
  nothing broke."
- Proof with nothing shipped: name what was observed instead ("Three hours in
  the build block, no output").
- Never leave a line blank and never pad one.
