# Workflow: Concept portfolio

**Produces:** a verdict on whether the surviving concepts compound, coexist,
conflict or distract, with each one placed as active, incubated or rejected, and
the shelf triggers written as observable events.

## Trigger

More than one concept survived convergence and all of them look attractive, or
the founder is quietly carrying three ideas at once and has never written them
on the same page. Also triggered on `/portfolio` and on `/incubate`.

## Steps

1. **List the live population.** Every concept currently alive (`BS-IDEA` with
   status candidate, surviving or selected), each with its genome, its frame of
   origin and its current surface decision.
2. **Extract the shared primitives.** For each pair, name the mechanism, asset,
   audience or capability they genuinely share. A shared word is not a shared
   primitive: "both are AI tools" is rejected, "both need the same permissioned
   data pipeline" is accepted.
3. **Classify every pair.** Exactly one of four:
   - **compound**: doing one makes the other cheaper, faster or more likely to
     succeed. Name the specific mechanism of the compounding.
   - **coexist**: independent, no meaningful interaction, and the only shared
     cost is attention.
   - **conflict**: they compete for the same scarce resource, or one's success
     makes the other harder or incoherent. Name the resource or the incoherence.
   - **distract**: one is materially weaker and its main effect is to consume
     the attention the stronger one needs.
4. **Write the coherence thesis.** One sentence that explains why this set
   belongs to the same founder or the same company. If no honest sentence
   exists, say so plainly: that absence is the finding, and it is a more useful
   output than a manufactured thesis.
5. **Cost the attention, not the money.** For each active concept state what it
   would consume in founder attention per week and for how long. Attention is
   the scarce resource this workflow actually allocates within the session; the
   allocation of money, people and calendar time across the whole company
   belongs to Strategy & Portfolio {OS}.
6. **Cut to the carried set.** Keep active only what the stated attention budget
   can genuinely carry. Everything else is incubated or rejected, never left
   ambiguously alive. An ambiguously alive concept is the most expensive object
   in a portfolio because it costs attention and returns nothing.
7. **Write the incubation records.** Each parked concept becomes a `BS-INC` with
   a resurrection trigger stated as an observable event: a named signal, a price
   crossing a threshold, a capability shipping, a regulation landing, a customer
   asking unprompted for the third time. "When we have more time" is not a
   trigger and is rejected.
8. **Record the conflicts as tensions.** A conflict pair that the founder
   refuses to resolve becomes a `BS-TEN` with both positions intact, not a
   silent preference.
9. **Hand the choice upward.** Where the real question is which concept gets
   funded against everything else the company is doing, say so and route it to
   Strategy & Portfolio {OS}. This workflow reports coherence; it does not
   allocate capital.
10. **Commit.** Write the active set, the shelf, the coherence thesis, the
    shared primitives and the conflicts to canonical state.

## Completion test

- Every live concept appears exactly once, with a status of active, incubated or
  rejected. Nothing is ambiguously alive.
- Every pair carries exactly one classification: compound, coexist, conflict or
  distract.
- Every claimed compounding names its mechanism. Every claimed conflict names
  the contested resource or the incoherence.
- Shared primitives are mechanisms, assets, audiences or capabilities, never
  category words.
- The coherence thesis is one sentence, or its absence is stated explicitly.
- Every incubated concept has a resurrection trigger that is an observable
  event, with no time-based placeholders.
- The active set fits the stated attention budget, and the budget is written
  down.
- Unresolved conflicts appear as `BS-TEN` with both positions intact.
- Any funding question is routed to Strategy & Portfolio {OS} rather than
  answered here.
