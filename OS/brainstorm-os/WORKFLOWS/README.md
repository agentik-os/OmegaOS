# Brainstorm {OS}: Workflows

Repeatable processes this OS runs. Each workflow is one file, named for what it
produces, and states its trigger, steps, and completion test.

| Workflow | Trigger | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [selected-concept.md](selected-concept.md) | an idea exists, a choice is due, and nobody has produced more than one real option | one concept chosen out of a genuinely generated and genuinely attacked population, with the dissent recorded beside the recommendation |
| [red-team-pass.md](red-team-pass.md) | a concept is leading and someone is about to commit: a freeze, a handoff, a spend, a quarter | ranked failure modes, each with a repair, an experiment or a kill recommendation, plus kill criteria written before the result exists |
| [concept-portfolio.md](concept-portfolio.md) | more than one concept survived and all of them look attractive | the compound / coexist / conflict / distract verdict per pair, the carried active set, and the incubation shelf with observable triggers |
| [frozen-handoff-packet.md](frozen-handoff-packet.md) | a session reached `BRAINSTORM CONVERGED: HANDOFF READY` | the immutable versioned concept, the packet cut to the named downstream unit, and the emitted events |
