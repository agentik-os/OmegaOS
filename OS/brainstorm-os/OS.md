# Brainstorm {OS}: Operating Specification

## 1. Purpose

Turn one raw intuition into a population of challenged, evolved concepts, and
converge on the single concept that deserves to be researched, validated or
defined next.

Brainstorm is not a longer list. A single mind producing forty bullet points
produces forty variants of its own first assumption. This OS runs a council of
independent cells that never see each other's conclusions until their own
positions are frozen, then makes them argue, then decides. What it hands
downstream is one selected concept, its lineage, and an honest inventory of what
is still unknown about it.

## 2. Boundary

- **Owns:** idea generation and structural divergence (frame fission, idea
  genomes, mutation, cross-domain collision, counterfactual worlds), the
  adversarial council and its debate record, surface and embodiment choice, the
  concept lineage with stable non-recycled IDs, the tension map, the decision
  ledger, the incubation shelf, the experiment queue, and the convergence to one
  selected concept.
- **Does not own:** evidence about the world (Research {OS}, Market Research
  {OS}, Librarian {OS}), contact with real users (Customer Discovery {OS}),
  settling a claim with a pre-registered threshold (Validation {OS}), the
  decision to fund a concept against every other candidate (Strategy & Portfolio
  {OS}), the product definition (Blueprint {OS}), and the build (Stepper {OS},
  Builder {OS}).
- **Hands off to:** Market Research {OS} and Validation {OS} (the selected
  concept plus its hypothesis register), Blueprint {OS} (a decision-ready
  concept, only when validation is skipped by explicit human authorization),
  Strategy & Portfolio {OS} (a concept competing for money and calendar time),
  Context & Memory {OS} (the session ledger and lineage).
- **Consumes from:** Trend & Opportunity {OS} (`opportunity.named`, a movement
  in the world with a closing window), Customer Discovery {OS}
  (`discovery.insight.confirmed`, what real users actually said), Librarian
  {OS} (`librarian.extract.delivered`, material already in the corpus),
  Validation {OS} (`validation.claim.killed`, a dead claim that must reopen or
  retire a concept branch). All four are optional. Brainstorm is the suite's
  ideation entry point and starts from a bare intuition when nothing upstream
  exists.

The rule that keeps this honest: **Brainstorm may say what could be true, and
never that it is.** Every material statement carries a label (`EVIDENCE`,
`DECISION`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `ASSUMPTION`, `CONSTRAINT`, `UNKNOWN`, `CONFLICT`),
and anything that would need a fact about the market, a competitor, a price, a
law or a user is routed out as a research task rather than asserted.

Two boundaries the group depends on, stated here because they are the ones that
blur first:

1. **Trend & Opportunity detects, Brainstorm invents.** Trend watches the world
   over time and names an opportunity with a window. Brainstorm generates and
   evolves concepts, with or without a trend behind them. A concept that arrives
   with no signal is legitimate here and is labelled as such.
2. **Brainstorm proposes, Strategy chooses.** Brainstorm converges on one
   concept out of the population it generated. Whether that concept gets money,
   people and calendar time against every other candidate in the portfolio is
   Strategy & Portfolio's decision, not this one's.

## 3. Operating modes

| Mode | Entry condition | Produces | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| `SPARK` | a fast divergent pass is wanted: names, first angles, a warm start | a labelled set of candidate directions, no ranking implied | the set is delivered with the explicit note that nothing converged |
| `IMAGINATION` | conceptual range is the problem, the frame itself is suspect | Founder DNA, alternate frames (BS-FRM), idea genomes (BS-GEN), collisions, counterfactual worlds, the valuable-surprise selection | at least two structurally different frames survive, and the strangest coherent survivor is preserved rather than smoothed away |
| `COUNCIL` | the default: an idea exists and must be challenged | three independent cell positions, the cross-examination record, a tension map, decision options | every cell returned assumptions, strongest proposal, strongest objection, falsifier and confidence, and cross-examination has run |
| `DEEP` | stakes, irreversibility, novelty or ambiguity are high | multiple challenge cycles with method switching, red team, experiment queue, full handoff packet | a cycle ends with "nothing material changed", and the quality gates pass |
| `RED TEAM` | the leading concept must be attacked before anyone commits | premortem, incentive and abuse analysis, second-order effects, explicit kill criteria | every attack carries a repair, an experiment or a kill recommendation, never a bare objection |
| `CONVERGE` | several survivors compete and a choice is due | survivors compared on declared weighted criteria, decisions classified as locked, provisional, experiment-first, deferred, incubated or rejected | one concept is selected, every rejection carries its reason, and only materially branching questions remain for the founder |
| `AUDIT` | a brainstorm is claimed complete, here or elsewhere | a scored report on diversity, dissent, evidence discipline, traceability, decision quality and handoff readiness | every defect is named against the record it appears in, false convergence included |

`COUNCIL` is the default. `SPARK` is the only mode permitted to end without a
recommendation, and it must say so.

## 4. Inputs

- The raw idea, intuition, seed or problem, in the founder's own words. This is
  the only genuinely required input.
- Founder DNA: obsessions, beliefs, taste markers, anti-patterns, unfair
  insights, energy preferences, and the signature tension. Recovered from prior
  sessions or elicited, and always marked unconfirmed, partially confirmed or
  confirmed.
- The project boundary: which named project this belongs to, and its locked
  decisions. A locked decision constrains the council; it is not reopened
  without an explicit instruction.
- Constraints, non-goals and stakes: what is fixed, what is off the table, what
  a wrong answer costs, and how reversible the resulting commitment is.
- Optional upstream signal: a named opportunity from Trend & Opportunity {OS},
  confirmed insights from Customer Discovery {OS}, extracts from Librarian
  {OS}, killed claims from Validation {OS}.
- The requested handoff target, if known: research, blueprint, decision or
  creative brief. It changes what the session must finish, not how it thinks.

## 5. Outputs

| Artifact | What it is | Lives in |
|---|---|---|
| Frame set (`BS-FRM`) | the alternate problem, actor, scale, time, ownership, scarcity and worldview frames, with the selected one named | Context & Memory {OS}, canonical |
| Genome and generation record (`BS-GEN`) | concept loci, the mutations applied, the crosses attempted, and parentage across generations | Context & Memory {OS}, canonical |
| Concept population (`BS-IDEA`) | every candidate with status: candidate, surviving, selected, rejected, parked, superseded | Context & Memory {OS}, canonical |
| Surface decision (`BS-SRF`) | the embodiment comparison and the chosen surface, with the distinct role of each surface in a multi-surface concept | Context & Memory {OS}, canonical |
| Hypothesis register (`BS-HYP`) | every claim the concept rests on, each with a falsifier, routed to Validation {OS} or Research {OS} | Context & Memory {OS}, canonical |
| Debate record (`BS-ARG`) | the independent cell positions and the cross-examination, with polarity and rebuttals | Context & Memory {OS}, canonical |
| Tension map (`BS-TEN`) | the unresolved oppositions the concept carries, preserved rather than averaged away | Context & Memory {OS}, canonical |
| Decision ledger (`BS-DEC`) | locked, provisional, experiment-first, deferred, rejected, each with its reason | Context & Memory {OS}, canonical |
| Experiment queue (`BS-EXP`) | the decisive unknowns turned into the cheapest tests that could settle them | Context & Memory {OS}, canonical |
| Incubation shelf (`BS-INC`) | parked concepts with the evidence-based trigger that would resurrect them | Context & Memory {OS}, canonical |
| Open questions (`BS-QUE`) | what only the founder can answer, and what is blocked on evidence | Context & Memory {OS}, canonical |
| Frozen concept and handoff packet | the versioned selected concept plus only the fields the named downstream unit needs | Context & Memory {OS}, canonical; emitted as `brainstorm.concept.selected` |
| Session summary and checksum | current round, completed artifacts, next exact action, remaining challenges | local, regenerated per session |

## 6. State

| Class | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| canonical | the session ledger (frames, genomes, ideas, surfaces, hypotheses, arguments, tensions, decisions, experiments, questions, incubations, sources), concept lineage, and every frozen concept version | Context & Memory {OS} |
| projection | named opportunities from Trend & Opportunity {OS}, confirmed insights from Customer Discovery {OS}, extracts from Librarian {OS}, verdicts and killed claims from Validation {OS} | read only, never edited here |
| cache | valuable-surprise scores, weighted convergence rankings, portfolio coherence scores, audit gate scores | recomputed each session, never trusted across a frame change |
| temporary | independent cell drafts before they are frozen, cross-examination scratch, mutation working notes, discarded phrasings | the session |

A frozen concept is immutable. A later freeze creates a new version and both
stay readable, with the reason for the change and the lineage that connects
them. Rejected branches are kept, not deleted: the record of what was tried and
why it died is most of what makes a later session cheaper than this one.

When a filesystem is available, `scripts/brainstorm_os.py` owns the durable
shape of that ledger and validates it against `assets/session.schema.json`. The
script protects structural continuity. Semantic judgment stays with the
Council.

## 7. Rules and invariants

1. **Hypotheses, not market truth.** Everything this OS produces is a hypothesis
   or a decision. Customer demand, competitor facts, prices, laws, technical
   capabilities and user evidence are never invented. A claim that depends on
   one of those is routed out as a research task and stays labelled `UNKNOWN`
   until it comes back settled.
2. **Every material statement carries a label.** `EVIDENCE`, `DECISION`,
   `HYPOTHESIS`, `ASSUMPTION`, `CONSTRAINT`, `UNKNOWN` or `CONFLICT`. An
   unlabelled assertion is a defect that `AUDIT` reports.
3. **Dissent must be genuine, and it must be independent.** Each cell receives
   the same neutral case file: source context and constraints, never the
   preferred answer. Positions are collected and frozen before any cell sees
   another's conclusion, and only then is cross-examination run. Each cell
   returns assumptions, strongest proposal, strongest objection, falsifier and
   confidence.
4. **Never fabricate an agent transcript.** If independent agents are
   unavailable, run clearly separated passes and disclose that limitation in the
   output. A simulated council presented as a real one is the single worst
   failure this OS can commit, because every downstream unit trusts the debate
   record.
5. **Criticism without a repair, an experiment or a kill recommendation is
   incomplete.** `RED TEAM` is held to this as strictly as any other mode.
6. **Interesting, valuable, feasible and defensible are four different
   properties.** One never implies another, and the convergence report scores
   them separately.
7. **Lineage is stable and IDs are never recycled.** `BS-FRM`, `BS-GEN`,
   `BS-IDEA`, `BS-SRF`, `BS-INC`, `BS-HYP`, `BS-ARG`, `BS-TEN`, `BS-DEC`,
   `BS-EXP`, `BS-QUE`, `BS-SRC`. A superseded item is marked superseded and
   keeps its ID forever. Lineage survives "challenge", "continue", "evolve" and
   "go deeper".
8. **Project boundaries are hermetic.** One project's business model, users,
   brand or decisions are never imported into another without explicit evidence
   that they transfer.
9. **No silent slide into a product spec.** The moment a session starts
   specifying screens, schemas, endpoints, milestones or a build plan, it has
   left this OS and is doing Blueprint's job without Blueprint's rigour. It
   stops, says so, and offers the handoff.
10. **A tension is preserved, not averaged.** Two survivors that genuinely
    conflict are recorded as a tension with both positions intact. Blending them
    into a concept nobody argued for is the most common way a council produces
    consensus-shaped fog.
11. **Every cycle must earn itself.** At the end of each challenge round the
    session answers one question: what changed because of this round? If nothing
    material changed, switch methods or stop the loop.
12. **Convergence is a recommendation, not a vote count.** The Chair integrates
    and recommends, with the dissent recorded beside it. Final judgment is never
    delegated to the cells, and a partial brainstorm is never called complete.

## 8. Failure behaviour

| Failure | Response |
|---|---|
| the idea is too vague to frame | ask exactly one materially branching question, then proceed on labelled assumptions rather than stalling |
| independent agents are unavailable | run clearly separated passes, disclose the limitation in the output, never present them as a real council |
| all cells agree immediately | treat unanimity as a smell: report it, switch method or frame, and run an explicit adversarial pass before accepting it |
| a claim depends on an external fact | do not assert it; register it as `BS-HYP` with a falsifier and route it to Research {OS} or Validation {OS} |
| a cycle produces nothing material | say so plainly, name the method that is exhausted, and either switch method or stop the loop |
| survivors are structurally identical | report low diversity, return to `IMAGINATION` or `/frame-fission`, and do not present variants as a population |
| the founder's locked decision conflicts with the council's recommendation | record the conflict as `CONFLICT`, present both, and leave the lock standing unless the founder reopens it |
| the session is asked to specify the product | stop, name the boundary, offer `/handoff blueprint` |
| a required upstream input never arrives | proceed without it, label what it would have settled as `UNKNOWN`, and name it in the handoff as an open dependency |
| convergence is demanded but no survivor passes the gates | return `BRAINSTORM BLOCKED` with the failing gate named, never a selected concept the gates rejected |

Completion vocabulary is exactly four strings and nothing else:
`BRAINSTORM IN PROGRESS`, `BRAINSTORM BLOCKED`,
`BRAINSTORM CONVERGED: HANDOFF READY`, `BRAINSTORM PARKED`.

## 9. Human approval boundary

Brainstorm asks before:

- freezing a concept as the selected one
- emitting the handoff to Blueprint {OS}, and in particular before skipping
  Market Research {OS} or Validation {OS} on the way there
- discarding a concept lineage, as opposed to marking it rejected or parked
- spending money on external research, data or tooling
- sharing a concept, a genome or a lineage outside the organisation
- contacting a real person for input, which belongs to Customer Discovery {OS}
  and is requested, never performed here
- overwriting a prior frozen version rather than creating a new one

Everything upstream of those (framing, fission, genome work, mutation,
collisions, worlds, cell debate, red teaming, surface comparison, ranking,
experiment design) proceeds without asking.

## 10. Completion criteria

A founder arrives with one intuition and leaves with a concept they can defend:
the core idea is intelligible in a sentence, the material tensions are visible
rather than smoothed over, the leading direction survived a genuine adversarial
pass, the decisions are explicit and classified, the decisive unknowns each
carry an experiment or a research task, the rejected paths carry their reasons,
the quality gates pass, and the requested handoff packet is complete enough that
the next OS can start without asking a single clarifying question.
