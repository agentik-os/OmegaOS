# Mindset {OS}

Identity and beliefs: the behavioural compiler under Alignment.

## When to use this

Reach for this OS when the user says something like:

- "I keep starting things and never finishing them."
- "I want to think like someone who runs a real business."
- "I know what to do and I do not do it."
- "Build me a personal operating system", "audit my life", "where do I stand".
- "Give me a Jim Rohn plan", "compile my personal philosophy".
- "Score my week", "coach me on the last month of follow-ups".
- "Set up the six-month identity challenge."

Near neighbours, and the one line that separates them:

| Confused with | Discriminator |
|---|---|
| Identity Shift {OS} (`identity-shift-os`) | it runs one bounded transition with a named from, a named to and a close-by date. This OS holds the identity you have today, with no end date. |
| Alignment {OS} (`alignment-os`) | it owns values, what you have chosen to hold as important. This OS owns beliefs, what you currently hold as true. |
| Goal & Life Strategy {OS} (`goal-life-strategy-os`) | it owns outcomes and deadlines. This OS owns the standards that produce them. |
| Habit Tracker {OS} (`habit-tracker-os`) | it owns the behaviour contract and the evidence it happened. This OS owns what that behaviour is supposed to prove about the person. |
| Health & Energy {OS} (`health-energy-os`) | it owns capacity and may veto a load. This OS designs the standard the load serves. |
| Journal {OS} (`journal-os`) | it captures and proposes patterns. This OS decides whether a pattern becomes a stated belief. |

## Capabilities

- Assesses a baseline across 12 life domains and marks what is unknown rather
  than guessing it.
- Writes identity statements as behaviours under conditions, each with an
  evidence test.
- Maintains a belief ledger with dated evidence for and against each belief.
- Compiles a personal philosophy constitution: inputs, principles, decision
  laws.
- Labels every claim E1, E2, S, P or C so a promising practice is never read as
  established fact.
- Runs a coaching pass over the latest daily, weekly or monthly follow-ups and
  returns exactly one keystone adjustment.
- Creates and validates a deterministic workspace on disk through
  `omega-mindset new`, `omega-mindset score`, `omega-mindset challenge` and
  `omega-mindset coach`.
- Routes clinical, crisis and medical territory to a qualified human
  professional and stops.

## Procedure

1. Check safety first. If there is any sign of acute distress, sleep collapse,
   unsafe behaviour, mania-like activation, substance instability or
   eating-disorder risk, run `STABILIZE`, surface the safety reference and route
   to a qualified professional. Do not build an ambitious plan over an unstable
   base.
2. Contract the outcome. State what will be different, by when, and what is
   explicitly out of scope. Separate desired outcomes, controllable processes,
   identity standards, non-negotiables and anti-goals.
3. Establish the baseline at the minimum useful depth: quick (five questions and
   a 12-domain score), standard (audit plus calendar, energy, money behaviour,
   environment) or deep. Mark every unknown as unknown.
4. Read the value set from Alignment {OS} and the behaviour evidence from Habit
   Tracker {OS}. If either is absent, say so and continue with that half flagged
   as unvalidated.
5. Produce the identity stack: meaning, values, roles, identity principles,
   standards, decisions, repeated actions, evidence, self-trust. Every identity
   statement names a condition and an observable action.
6. Compile the personal philosophy when recurring results trace back to
   assumptions rather than effort: inputs, philosophy, attitude, activity,
   results, lifestyle, review, updated philosophy.
7. Install the minimum effective behaviour. One primary identity standard, one
   energy keystone, one value-creation behaviour, one relationship or chosen
   practice, one measurement ritual. Everything else goes to `NOT NOW`.
8. Hand each behaviour to Habit Tracker {OS} as a contract, and each outcome to
   Goal & Life Strategy {OS} as a goal. Do not keep either here.
9. Run the cycle, then review: promises kept, quality of process, energy,
   value created, avoidance, friction, integrity cost, next experiment. Score
   the week with `omega-mindset score`.
10. Update the belief ledger from evidence, not from one emotional day. Record
    what changed, the date, and the evidence that moved it.

## Handoffs

| Receiving OS | What it receives | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Identity Shift {OS} (`identity-shift-os`) | a shift request | current identity named, target identity named, and the reason this does not fit inside a standing belief update |
| Habit Tracker {OS} (`habit-tracker-os`) | a behaviour contract | one trigger, one action, one evidence test, one floor version that survives a bad day |
| Goal & Life Strategy {OS} (`goal-life-strategy-os`) | an outcome | the identity standard it serves, the lead indicator, and the horizon |
| Decision {OS} (`decision-os`) | a hard call | the framing, the identity standards that bear on it, and the belief that is currently deciding it by default |
| Context & Memory {OS} (`context-memory-os`) | the canonical identity model | adopted identity statements, the belief ledger with dates, and the personal philosophy |

Incoming: Identity Shift {OS} hands back the closing identity model when a shift
ends, and this OS adopts it as the new standing model or states plainly why it
declines.
