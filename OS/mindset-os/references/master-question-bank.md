## 1. Usage rules

- Ask only questions that can change a decision, protocol, or interpretation.
- Use three to seven questions per coaching round.
- Draft assumptions first when the user requests autonomy.
- Never turn the entire bank into an intake form.
- Treat answers as current hypotheses, not permanent identity facts.

## 2. Philosophy

- What rule seems to govern your life even though you never chose it?
- Which belief has produced the same result three times?
- What do you believe must happen before you are allowed to feel successful?
- Which principle works in emergencies but damages normal life?
- What did your family teach you about money without saying it directly?
- What did school teach you about intelligence, permission, and failure?
- What does your faith teach you about ambition, stewardship, rest, and service?
- Which mentor or creator occupies too much authority in your mind?
- What evidence would make you revise a core belief?
- Where are you using a slogan instead of a causal explanation?
- What truth would simplify your life but force a difficult decision?
- Which principle do you want to remain true even if nobody applauds?
- What do you believe about people who become wealthy?
- What do you believe about people who stay poor?
- Which of those beliefs is unfair, incomplete, or dangerous?
- What is the difference between responsibility and blame in your philosophy?
- What is your rule for reversible versus irreversible decisions?
- What is your rule for stopping something that no longer works?
- What is your rule for repair after violating a value?
- What philosophy should your calendar prove this week?

## 3. Attitude and emotional orientation

- Do you treat the past as evidence, identity, debt, teacher, or prison?
- What future outcome creates hope? Which creates pressure or grandiosity?
- What emotion are you trying to earn through money?
- What emotion do you refuse to feel while acting?
- What does rejection mean about you?
- What does another person's success activate?
- Where does optimism become denial?
- Where does realism become cynicism?
- What attitude would honor both the facts and your agency?
- What do you need to grieve instead of optimize?
- What can you appreciate without pretending everything is good?
- Which relationship to yourself creates your current standards?
- What self-talk increases responsibility without cruelty?
- When does enthusiasm make you overcommit?
- Which bodily signs indicate fear, shame, overload, or activation?

## 4. Activity and discipline

- What did you actually complete in the last seven days?
- Which activity most directly creates value?
- Which activity mainly creates the feeling of progress?
- What are you preparing for instead of doing?
- What is easy to do and easy to neglect in your current season?
- Which three disciplines would create the greatest divergence over a year?
- Which repeated error quietly compounds against you?
- What is the floor version on a bad day?
- Which cue and environment will make the action more likely?
- What will be removed to make room?
- What does “done” mean before you start?
- What must happen before a new project can activate?
- What is the smallest honest promise you can keep today?
- Are you using discipline to serve life or to punish yourself?
- What activity requires support, delegation, treatment, or a different system rather than more willpower?

## 5. Results and feedback

- Which results are improving, flat, or worsening?
- What is the baseline and how confident are you?
- Which lead action should plausibly affect the result?
- What external condition or luck variable mattered?
- Did the plan fail, or was it not actually executed enough to test?
- What cost accompanied the result?
- What result are you explaining away?
- What good process produced a bad short-term outcome?
- What bad process received a lucky outcome?
- What would make you continue, pivot, or stop?
- Which metric could be gamed?
- What feedback source is missing?
- How quickly can the next experiment return evidence?
- What result deserves celebration and preservation?
- What lesson should update your philosophy?

## 6. Lifestyle and enoughness

- What does a rich ordinary Tuesday look like?
- Which parts of that life are available now?
- Which experiences truly require more money?
- What does luxury mean to you: quality, beauty, privacy, access, status, or care?
- What spending is nourishment and what spending is performance?
- What is enough income, liquidity, net worth, freedom, and time?
- What would you keep doing after financial independence?
- What should wealth amplify in your character?
- What do you never want wealth to amplify?
- Which relationships belong in your future lifestyle?
- Where do faith, art, music, travel, hospitality, nature, and service fit?
- What form of rest has no productivity justification?
- What lifestyle upgrade requires a financial rule before permission?
- What small elegance or generosity can happen this week?
- If the goal takes ten years, how will you live during the journey?

## 7. Identity, purpose, and faith

- Who are you when no project is succeeding?
- What role receives too much of your identity?
- What promise would restore the most self-trust?
- Who benefits from the person you are becoming?
- What must never be sacrificed to reach the target?
- What does stewardship mean in behavior?
- What is your season asking you to build, heal, learn, release, or protect?
- What do you believe you are called to contribute?
- Where does prayer deepen responsibility?
- Where might spirituality be helping you avoid evidence or grief?
- Which value becomes real only when it costs something?
- What identity standard survives travel, fatigue, and disappointment?
- What part of the future identity is already evidenced?
- What part is still fantasy without behavior?
- What repair would bring identity and reality closer today?

## 8. Goals and seasons

- Which goals concern becoming, learning, creating, giving, owning, and experiencing?
- Which goal is borrowed from comparison?
- Is the financial target income, profit, liquidity, net worth, or enterprise value?
- Why this number and this deadline?
- What causal system must exist first?
- Which one goal earns the next 90 days?
- What will move to Not Now?
- What is the weekly visible result?
- What obstacle inside you is most likely?
- What if-then plan will answer it?
- Which domain is in winter, spring, summer, or autumn?
- Are you planting in a domain that currently requires protection or recovery?
- What opportunity has an expiration date?
- What progress needs protecting through the middle?
- What harvest needs measuring, allocating, sharing, or ending?

## 9. Learning and journal

- What live problem should your learning solve?
- Which capability is the current bottleneck?
- What source has primary evidence or verified experience?
- How will you practice rather than consume?
- Who or what will provide feedback?
- What will be shipped or explained each week?
- What subject is fascinating but Not Now?
- Which lesson have you learned repeatedly but not operationalized?
- What deserves a `LESSON`, `DECISION`, `ERROR`, `WIN`, or `QUESTION` entry?
- Which journal note should become a principle?
- Which principle should become a behavior?
- What result would prove the skill improved?
- What should be taught to consolidate learning?
- When does journaling become avoidance?
- What should be reread monthly or annually?

## 10. Marketplace value and enterprise

- Which painful, frequent, urgent, valuable problem can you solve?
- Who recognizes the problem and can pay or act?
- What evidence shows demand?
- What valuable outcome do customers actually buy?
- What capability would make you harder to replace?
- What trust signal or proof is missing?
- Does your offer describe activity or outcome?
- Which distribution behavior is being avoided?
- What would increase value without adding hours?
- What can be standardized, delegated, automated, productized, or owned?
- What should remain human judgment?
- What is wage/time income versus profit/ownership income today?
- Which small enterprise or asset can be completed in 90 days?
- What margin, quality, or capacity constraint will appear if sales grow?
- How will service and integrity remain visible at scale?

## 11. Money and stewardship

- How much comes in, goes out, is owed, reserved, owned, and at risk?
- What numbers are facts and what numbers are estimates?
- Who gets paid first by your current behavior?
- Is allocation decided before money arrives?
- What belongs to taxes, essentials, runway, reinvestment, long-term capital, giving, and lifestyle?
- What debt or concentration threatens freedom?
- What purchase is trying to create identity?
- What lifestyle inflation rule would preserve optionality?
- What return promise or urgency should trigger a pause?
- Which decision needs legal, tax, or financial expertise?
- What risk could remove you from the game?
- What is the base, upside, and downside scenario?
- What is your relationship to generosity under abundance and scarcity?
- What would ethical wealth stewardship look like at 1M, 10M, and 1B?
- How will you retain access to uncomfortable truth as wealth grows?

## 12. Associations and relationships

- Who receives most of your real attention?
- How do you feel and behave after repeated contact?
- What standards does each environment normalize?
- Who tells you the truth with care?
- Who supports health, faith, focus, courage, and integrity?
- Which relationship needs gratitude or investment?
- Which relationship needs repair?
- Which relationship needs a boundary?
- Where are you staying from fear, status, guilt, or utility?
- Where are you treating people as leverage rather than persons?
- Which peers have the craft and character you want to learn?
- What circle is missing: intimate, peer, mentor, health, faith, culture, or business?
- Which five digital voices shape your philosophy most?
- Which input installs fear, cynicism, comparison, or reckless ambition?
- How will you diversify influence without abandoning loyalty or care?

## 13. Health, energy, and environment

- What is actual sleep opportunity over 14 days?
- Is your wake-time ambition compatible with bedtime reality?
- Which energy problem might require medical assessment?
- What food, hydration, movement, or recovery pattern is missing?
- Is exercise care, craft, punishment, or escape?
- What is the minimum safe physical floor?
- Which optional optimization is distracting from basics?
- What substances or digital behaviors regulate state for you?
- What environment makes the desired behavior obvious?
- Which friction can disappear tonight?
- What warning sign requires stopping and seeking help?
- Which relationships protect health?
- How will travel preserve the floor?
- How will a high-energy day avoid overcommitment?
- What is the recovery plan after a launch or sprint?

## 14. Founder and attention

- What is `Now`, `Next`, and `Not Now`?
- What active project has no clear definition of done?
- Which idea appeared because the current work became boring or vulnerable?
- What research needs a time box and decision artifact?
- Which task exists only in your head?
- What can be externalized, templated, or delegated?
- What part needs body doubling or accountability?
- Which tool, model, or framework novelty is becoming avoidance?
- What must be finished before exploration unlocks?
- What is the first two-minute launch action?
- Which communication window fragments your best attention?
- What work-in-progress limit will you enforce?
- What should be automated only after ten manual repetitions?
- What irreversible choice must remain yours?
- What completed asset would reduce dependence on your time?

## 15. Risk, failure, and legacy

- What can fail without threatening essentials?
- What failure would be difficult to recover from?
- What are the health, financial, legal, relationship, ethical, and reputation downsides?
- What signals indicate mania-like activation, burnout, addiction, or crisis?
- Who can interrupt you when judgment is impaired?
- What decision requires a waiting period?
- What did failure reveal about philosophy, activity, skill, system, market, or luck?
- What harm requires repair rather than reframing?
- What should be closed rather than resurrected?
- What will you be glad you protected even if the goal fails?
- What knowledge, assets, systems, relationships, and character should remain after you?
- What do you want loved ones, collaborators, customers, and community to experience from your success?
- What power or wealth might make harder to hear?
- What governance preserves truth and stewardship?
- What is the next courageous, bounded, ethical action?
