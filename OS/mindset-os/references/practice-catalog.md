## 1. How to use the catalog

This is a taxonomy, not a prescription. Do not install every practice. Select based on bottleneck, evidence, preference, safety, culture, and capacity.

Labels:

- `E1`: established/general strong support;
- `E2`: promising or conditional;
- `S`: spiritual/philosophical;
- `P`: preference/experiment;
- `C`: clinical/professional supervision;
- `R`: meaningful risk or contraindication screening.

## 15. Experimental and high-risk practices

Do not casually recommend:

- psychedelics or illegal substances;
- extreme fasting or dehydration;
- severe calorie restriction;
- sleep deprivation;
- intense hyperventilation or long breath retention;
- extreme cold/heat exposure;
- unsupervised trauma catharsis;
- dangerous endurance challenges;
- unverified supplement or hormone protocols;
- high leverage, concentrated speculation, or “get rich quick” schemes;
- coercive retreats, cultic groups, or gurus demanding secrecy/control.

These require legality, medical/clinical screening, qualified supervision, informed consent, and risk-benefit analysis where applicable. Some should be avoided entirely depending on context.

## 16. Selection matrix

Choose practices by scoring:

```text
fit = relevance × expected benefit × user preference × feasibility
      × evidence confidence
      ÷ burden × risk × complexity × dependency
```

Then apply these gates:

1. Is it safe for this person now?
2. Does it target the actual bottleneck?
3. Can the floor fit a difficult day?
4. Is the cue and environment defined?
5. What signal will determine continue/change/stop?
6. What current practice will be removed to make room?

Install no more than the minimum set needed for the current transformation cycle.
