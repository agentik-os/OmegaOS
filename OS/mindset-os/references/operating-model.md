## 1. Purpose and boundaries

Mindset {OS} turns aspirations into a repeatable personal system. It integrates meaning, identity, psychological skills, physical energy, behavior design, wealth creation, relationships, and feedback.

It is not psychotherapy, medical treatment, financial advice, a religion, or a guarantee of success. It can complement qualified care and the user's chosen faith.

## 2. System map

```text
                    MEANING / FAITH / VALUES
                              |
                   IDENTITY / SELF-STORY
                              |
      +-----------------------+-----------------------+
      |                       |                       |
  MIND & EMOTION          BODY & ENERGY          RELATIONSHIPS
      |                       |                       |
      +-----------------------+-----------------------+
                              |
                  ATTENTION / DECISIONS / HABITS
                              |
                  WORK / VALUE / OWNERSHIP / MONEY
                              |
                    OUTCOMES / EVIDENCE / LEARNING
                              |

```

The system must remain circular. Identity informs action; evidence updates identity. A person does not wait to “feel like” the future self before generating evidence.

## 3. Transformation layers

### Layer 0 — Safety and stability

Protect sleep, basic nutrition, physical safety, medication adherence, sobriety where relevant, and access to professional care. Acute instability overrides optimization.

### Layer 1 — Awareness

Observe present reality without moralizing: time, energy, thoughts, emotions, money, relationships, behavior, environment, and results.

### Layer 2 — Meaning

Clarify what makes wealth worth pursuing, what must never be sacrificed, whom the user wants to serve, and what “enough” means.

### Layer 3 — Identity

Define chosen roles, principles, standards, boundaries, and evidence. Remove identities based purely on status, comparison, or fantasy.

### Layer 4 — Regulation

Build the capacity to notice, tolerate, and direct attention and emotion. Regulation is not suppression; it creates choice.

### Layer 5 — Energy

Stabilize sleep, movement, food, recovery, breath, light, and substances. Energy is infrastructure for judgment and consistency.

### Layer 6 — Execution

Convert strategy into one decisive outcome, scheduled actions, stable cues, friction design, and feedback.

### Layer 7 — Wealth and contribution

Create useful value, communicate it, distribute it, own assets or equity, allocate resources, manage risk, and compound.

### Layer 8 — Integration

Review outcomes and costs. Update beliefs, systems, identity, and strategy. Preserve humility as capability grows.

## 4. State model

Classify the user's current state before selecting intensity:

| State | Signals | Coaching response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `RED` | acute risk, severe sleep loss, mania-like activation, self-harm, medical warning | stabilize and refer; no performance escalation |
| `AMBER` | chronic overwhelm, frequent crashes, poor sleep, financial panic, relapse risk | floor routines, reduce load, restore support |
| `GREEN` | stable basics, workable capacity, realistic ambition | standard 30/90-day build |
| `BLUE` | stable high performance with recovery and reliable systems | deepen leverage, leadership, stewardship |

Never infer `BLUE` from enthusiasm, long work hours, grand plans, or high income alone.

## 5. Design principles

### Systems before willpower

Inspect cues, friction, commitments, calendar, energy, and social environment before prescribing discipline.

### Evidence before affirmation

Use affirmations to direct attention and rehearse standards. Build self-trust from promises kept and honest repair.

### Process before outcome fantasy

Visualize the work, obstacles, coping, and next step more than the trophy.

### Floors before streaks

Define a version that can be completed on difficult days. Never make a streak the source of identity or shame.

### Seasons before balance mythology

Life domains need minimum protection, but not equal time every day. Define the current season and what maintenance means elsewhere.

### Truth before positivity

Optimism must coexist with cash reality, health data, customer feedback, opportunity cost, and downside scenarios.

### Service before status

The cleanest wealth identity asks: “What valuable outcome can I help create, at what scale, with what integrity?”

## 6. Wealth-specific logic

Use this causal chain:

```text
SELF-REGULATION
  -> RELIABLE EXECUTION
    -> RARE / VALUABLE CAPABILITY
      -> VALUE CREATION
        -> COMMUNICATION + DISTRIBUTION
          -> OWNERSHIP + LEVERAGE
            -> SURPLUS + CAPITAL ALLOCATION
              -> COMPOUNDING
                -> WEALTH (under uncertainty)
```

Mindset affects the chain but does not replace market demand, capital, legal structure, teams, technology, or luck.

## 7. Failure modes

- **Identity theater:** buying symbols or speaking in grandiose language without evidence.
- **Routine maximalism:** installing 15 habits and failing by day four.
- **Spiritual bypassing:** using positivity or prayer to avoid grief, conflict, data, or treatment.
- **Manifestation overconfidence:** confusing optimism with probability.
- **Founder martyrdom:** treating sleep deprivation and isolation as ambition.
- **Metric obsession:** reducing worth to streaks, revenue, weight, or productivity.
- **Perpetual preparation:** journaling and learning without shipping or selling.
- **Premature automation:** systemizing an unproven behavior or business process.
- **All-or-nothing relapse:** interpreting one miss as permission to abandon the cycle.
- **Coach dependence:** outsourcing every decision instead of increasing judgment.

Counter every failure mode with a visible rule, a stop criterion, and a reset protocol.
