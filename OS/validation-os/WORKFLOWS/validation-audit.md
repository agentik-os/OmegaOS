# Workflow: Validation audit

**Produces:** a per-claim report of what an inherited artifact actually measured
versus what it asserts.

## Trigger

A deck, research pack, blueprint or investor update uses the word "validated",
"proven", "confirmed demand" or "product market fit", and you did not run the
test yourself.

## Steps

1. **Extract every validation claim in the artifact,** with the exact sentence
   and where it appears.
2. **For each, find the evidence it rests on.** If no evidence is cited, that is
   the finding; record it and move on rather than hunting for a charitable
   reading.
3. **Classify the evidence:** desk research, stated intent, observed behaviour,
   money moved, or none. Only observed behaviour and money moved can support the
   word validated.
4. **Check for a pre-registered threshold.** Was a success criterion set before
   the data? If it cannot be shown, the claim is unvalidated regardless of the
   result.
5. **Check sample and window.** Sample size, who was sampled, when, and whether
   the population matches the claim's subject. A claim about enterprise buyers
   supported by a survey of students fails here.
6. **Check for survivorship and selection.** Who was excluded, who did not
   respond, and whether the responders differ from the target in a way that
   matters.
7. **Restate each claim at the strength the evidence actually supports.** Not
   deleted, restated. "Validated demand" often becomes "14 people said they were
   interested in a concept description in March".
8. **List the gap claims:** the ones asserted at a strength the evidence cannot
   carry, ordered by how much of the plan rests on them.
9. **Register the gap claims** into the claim register so they can be tested
   properly, and hand the top one to the signed test spec workflow.

## Completion test

- Every use of validated, proven or confirmed in the artifact appears in the
  report with its source sentence.
- Each claim carries an evidence class and a restated strength.
- The report names which claims have a pre-registered threshold and which do
  not.
- The gap claims are in the claim register, owned, and ranked.
- The report says nothing about whether the idea is good. That is not this
  workflow's job.
