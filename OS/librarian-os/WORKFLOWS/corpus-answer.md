# Workflow: Corpus answer

**Produces:** an answer assembled only from indexed material, cited on every
claim, with the gaps named.

## Trigger

A question is asked that your own sources may already answer. This runs BEFORE
any outside search: Research {OS} should start from what the corpus already
settled.

## Steps

1. **Restate the question** and say what kind of answer would satisfy it: a
   definition, a mechanism, a number, a method, a position in a debate.
2. **Retrieve candidates first, answer second.** Pull the extracts that bear on
   the question and list them before composing anything. Composing first and
   retrieving later is how invented citations happen.
3. **Filter by scope condition.** An extract whose scope condition does not
   cover the question's situation is dropped, and dropping it is noted.
4. **Compose the answer from the retrieved extracts only.** Every claim carries
   its citation with a locator. Nothing enters the answer that did not come from
   a retrieved extract.
5. **Mark the disagreements.** If the corpus contains conflicting positions,
   present both with attribution and name the axis they disagree on. Do not
   resolve a debate the sources did not resolve.
6. **Name the gaps explicitly.** What part of the question the corpus does not
   cover, and which sources are present but unindexed and therefore unsearched.
7. **Hand off the residue.** Package the unanswered part of the question, plus
   the part already answered, for Research {OS}. The point is that the outside
   search is now smaller and better aimed.
8. **File the answer** if it is durable enough to be asked again, so the next
   session retrieves it rather than recomputing it.

## Completion test

- Every sentence carrying a claim has a citation with a locator.
- No claim in the answer comes from outside the corpus.
- Conflicting sources appear as a conflict, attributed.
- The answer states what the corpus does not cover, and lists sources that are
  present but unsearchable.
- If nothing relevant was indexed, the answer says exactly that and nothing
  more.
