# Sales {OS}: Workflows

Repeatable processes this OS runs. Each workflow is one file, named for what it
produces, and states its trigger, steps, and completion test.

| Workflow | Trigger | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [Qualification verdict](qualification-verdict.md) | a lead arrives from inbound, a consented introduction, or outbound | a pursue, gather or disqualify verdict with every unknown recorded as unknown |
| [Closed won handoff](closed-won-handoff.md) | a deal reaches closed won | the agreed scope, terms and every promise made, emitted to Revenue and Delivery |
| [Loss review](loss-review.md) | an opportunity is marked closed lost | the stated reason, the believed reason, the real loss stage and the owning unit |
