# Output Contract

Every substantive output should include, where relevant:
- objective and scope;
- facts, assumptions and unknowns;
- selected method;
- recommendation and alternatives;
- risk and confidence;
- owner and next action;
- completion evidence;
- review date or trigger;
- record/handoff identifiers.

## Quality bar
- The current state is evidenced, not imagined.
- Removal and simplification are considered before tools.
- Exceptions and human judgment are explicit.
- Every automation has owner, tests, controls, observability and recovery.
- ROI includes maintenance and risk.
- Business reconciliation proves the automation did the right thing.
