# Operations Interview Questionnaire

## Purpose and customer
What triggers the process? Who receives value? What does good completion look like?

## Real sequence
Walk through the last actual case. Which tools/files/messages were used? Where did work wait, repeat or require workaround?

## Decisions and exceptions
Which steps require judgment? What are the top exceptions? Missing/late/duplicate/incorrect/unauthorized inputs?

## Volume and performance
Frequency, cases per period, processing time, elapsed time, backlog, error/rework, service/SLA and cost.

## Controls and risk
Approvals, separation of duties, privacy/security/legal controls, audit evidence and manual fallback.

## Change and ownership
Owner, maintainers, upcoming changes, tools already available, prior automation failures and desired future state.
