# Automation Candidate Scorecard

Score 0–5 and attach evidence:
- frequency and volume
- active time and waiting reduction
- error/rework reduction
- customer/service gain
- rule stability
- structured data quality
- exception rate/complexity
- consequence/risk
- API/integration availability
- process change rate
- reversibility
- build and maintenance cost
- vendor lock-in
- measurable ROI

Decision: remove / simplify / standardize / delegate / assist / automate / reject. A high total never overrides a critical safety/control failure.
