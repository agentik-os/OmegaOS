# Curated Book Canon

This canon combines primary sources, recognized practitioner works and widely read/bestselling books. Inclusion is not an assertion that every claim in every book is scientifically established. The OS must label evidence accordingly.

## Systems and operating discipline
1. Michael Gerber — The E-Myth Revisited
2. Sam Carpenter — Work the System
3. David Jenyns — SYSTEMology
4. Mike Michalowicz — Clockwork
5. Atul Gawande — The Checklist Manifesto
6. Andy Grove — High Output Management
## Lean, flow and constraints
1. James Womack & Daniel Jones — Lean Thinking
2. Mike Rother & John Shook — Learning to See
3. Jeffrey Liker — The Toyota Way
4. Eliyahu Goldratt — The Goal
5. Gene Kim et al. — The Phoenix Project
## Automation and AI operating models
1. Marco Iansiti & Karim Lakhani — Competing in the Age of AI
2. Aytekin Tank — Automate Your Busywork
3. Tom Davenport & Julia Kirby — Only Humans Need Apply
4. Paul Daugherty & James Wilson — Human + Machine
## Technology and team design
1. Matthew Skelton & Manuel Pais — Team Topologies
2. Nicole Forsgren, Jez Humble & Gene Kim — Accelerate
3. Martin Kleppmann — Designing Data-Intensive Applications
4. Michael Nygard — Release It!
## Change and improvement
1. Chip & Dan Heath — Switch
2. John Kotter — Leading Change
3. Donella Meadows — Thinking in Systems
4. Peter Senge — The Fifth Discipline

## Reading rule
- Prefer primary standards and first-party research for current factual claims.
- Use books for models, language, history and practitioner insight.
- Never turn a bestselling label into proof that a claim is true.
