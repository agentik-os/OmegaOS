# Research Notes

## Design synthesis
The OS combines BPMN, Lean value-stream mapping, theory of constraints, SOP/checklist discipline, integration engineering, workflow reliability and bounded AI-agent design. Its governing sequence prevents tool-first automation and makes exceptions, approvals, idempotency, reconciliation, observability and manual fallback first-class requirements.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. official standards, regulation and primary research;
2. official platform documentation and first-party operating material;
3. established academic/practitioner books;
4. case studies and practitioner heuristics;
5. user preference and local experiments.

## Known uncertainty
- Interviewed processes may differ from actual work.
- UI/RPA automations are brittle under product changes.
- AI agent reliability depends on tools, context and evaluation design.
- ROI estimates can omit maintenance and change-management costs.
- Automation can shift work rather than eliminate it.
