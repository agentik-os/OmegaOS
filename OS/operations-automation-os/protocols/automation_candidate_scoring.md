# Protocol — Automation Candidate Scoring

Score 0–5: frequency, volume, time, error/rework, customer/service impact, rule stability, data quality, exception rate, risk, integration availability, process change rate, maintainability, reversibility and ROI. High value with high risk may still require human-in-loop or rejection.

## Required closure
- decision or output
- owner
- observable completion evidence
- review trigger
- memory/handoff instruction
