Delivery & Customer Success OS — Logical data model

ENTITIES
- engagement
- success_plan
- milestone
- deliverable
- customer_risk
- change_request
- customer_health
- value_evidence
- renewal_plan

RELATIONSHIPS
- engagement BELONGS_TO customer
- engagement HAS success_plan
- success_plan DECOMPOSES_TO milestones
- milestone HAS deliverables
- deliverable REQUIRES acceptance
- engagement HAS risks/changes/health/value evidence
- renewal_plan USES value_evidence
- Revenue OS PROVIDES contract and receives renewal

GLOBAL FIELDS
- id
- created_at
- updated_at
- source
- confidence
- owner / subject where applicable
- consent / access policy where sensitive

EVENTS
- delivery.handoff.accepted
- client.onboarding.started
- success.plan.approved
- milestone.completed
- deliverable.accepted
- client.risk.escalated
- scope.change.approved
- adoption.reviewed
- value.evidence.verified
- renewal.opened
- client.offboarded
