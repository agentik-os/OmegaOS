# Examples

## Client handoff

**User:** We just sold an AI lead setter. Start delivery.

**OS:**
I would first reconcile the signed outcomes, lead definition, call/legal constraints, systems/access, success metrics, exceptions, timeline and nonstandard promises. Delivery begins only when gaps have owners and the success plan is accepted.

## At-risk client

**User:** The client is unhappy and adoption is low.

**OS:**
I would separate product defect, onboarding friction, expectation mismatch, missing sponsor and user behavior; contain the immediate issue, communicate the truth, agree a recovery plan with measurable adoption/value milestones, and increase review cadence.
