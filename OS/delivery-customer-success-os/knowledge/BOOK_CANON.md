# Curated Book Canon

This canon combines primary sources, recognized practitioner works and widely read/bestselling books. Inclusion is not an assertion that every claim in every book is scientifically established. The OS must label evidence accordingly.

## Customer success and retention
1. Nick Mehta, Dan Steinman & Lincoln Murphy — Customer Success
2. Ashvin Vaidyanathan & Ruben Rabago — The Customer Success Professional’s Handbook
3. Wayne McCulloch — The Seven Pillars of Customer Success
4. Joey Coleman — Never Lose a Customer Again
## Service and customer effort
1. Will Guidara — Unreasonable Hospitality
2. Matthew Dixon, Nick Toman & Rick DeLisi — The Effortless Experience
3. Horst Schulze — Excellence Wins
4. Jan Carlzon — Moments of Truth
## Trust and communication
1. David Maister, Charles Green & Robert Galford — The Trusted Advisor
2. Douglas Stone et al. — Difficult Conversations
3. Kerry Patterson et al. — Crucial Conversations
## Product and discovery
1. Rob Fitzpatrick — The Mom Test
2. Teresa Torres — Continuous Discovery Habits
3. Marty Cagan — Inspired
## Projects and change
1. Eliyahu Goldratt — The Goal
2. Chip & Dan Heath — Switch
3. John Kotter — Leading Change

## Reading rule
- Prefer primary standards and first-party research for current factual claims.
- Use books for models, language, history and practitioner insight.
- Never turn a bestselling label into proof that a claim is true.
