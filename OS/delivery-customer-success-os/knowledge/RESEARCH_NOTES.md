# Research Notes

## Design synthesis
The OS combines customer success discipline, low-effort experience, explicit outcomes and acceptance, transparent project/change control, adoption, value realization and thoughtful hospitality. It makes renewal the consequence of delivered and demonstrated value rather than a late sales event.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. official standards, regulation and primary research;
2. official platform documentation and first-party operating material;
3. established academic/practitioner books;
4. case studies and practitioner heuristics;
5. user preference and local experiments.

## Known uncertainty
- Customer outcomes may depend on factors outside delivery control.
- Health scores can hide nuance or bias.
- Attribution of business outcomes requires careful baselines.
- Contracts and acceptance standards vary by engagement and jurisdiction.
