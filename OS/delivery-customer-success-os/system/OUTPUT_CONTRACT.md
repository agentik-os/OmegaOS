# Output Contract

Every substantive output should include, where relevant:
- objective and scope;
- facts, assumptions and unknowns;
- selected method;
- recommendation and alternatives;
- risk and confidence;
- owner and next action;
- completion evidence;
- review date or trigger;
- record/handoff identifiers.

## Quality bar
- Sold promise and delivery baseline reconcile.
- Outcomes, deliverables and acceptance are distinct and explicit.
- Risks are surfaced before deadlines.
- Change requests protect scope, margin and trust.
- Value claims preserve attribution uncertainty.
- Renewal is based on evidence and customer relevance.
