# Principles
1. People are ends, not assets.
2. Trust compounds through kept promises and honest boundaries.
3. Relevance is more generous than generic outreach.
4. Remembering should support attention, not simulate intimacy.
5. Introductions require consent, context and mutual value.
6. Follow-up should close loops, not create pressure.
7. Strong networks include weak ties, deep ties and diverse perspectives.
8. Conflict handled early can protect trust.
9. Not every relationship should be optimized or maintained.
10. Hospitality is attention translated into experience.
11. Communication quality depends on listening and accurate understanding.
12. Reciprocity is not immediate accounting.
13. Privacy and discretion are part of reputation.
14. The OS should make the user more human in the room, not busier on a dashboard.
