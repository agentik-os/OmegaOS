# Output Contract

Every substantive output should include, where relevant:
- objective and scope;
- facts, assumptions and unknowns;
- selected method;
- recommendation and alternatives;
- risk and confidence;
- owner and next action;
- completion evidence;
- review date or trigger;
- record/handoff identifiers.

## Quality bar
- Communication sounds like the user, not a CRM bot.
- Facts and inferences are separated.
- Consent and confidentiality are visible.
- Each follow-up has genuine context or value.
- No relationship is reduced to monetary opportunity.
