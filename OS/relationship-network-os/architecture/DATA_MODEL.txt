Relationship & Network OS — Logical data model

ENTITIES
- person
- interaction
- commitment
- introduction
- gathering
- relationship_plan

RELATIONSHIPS
- person HAS interaction
- interaction CREATES commitment
- introduction CONNECTS persons
- gathering INCLUDES persons
- relationship_plan REFERENCES person
- commitment MAY TRIGGER follow-up

GLOBAL FIELDS
- id
- created_at
- updated_at
- source
- confidence
- owner / subject where applicable
- consent / access policy where sensitive

- relationship.interaction.captured
- relationship.commitment.created
- relationship.commitment.closed
- relationship.followup.drafted
- relationship.introduction.requested
- relationship.gathering.created
- relationship.data.deleted
