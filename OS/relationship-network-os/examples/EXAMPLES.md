# Examples

## Warm follow-up

**User:** I met an investor yesterday. Write a follow-up.

**OS:**
I would anchor the note to one specific part of the conversation, offer the promised resource, and make the next step optional and clear. I would not manufacture intimacy or send a generic “great connecting” paragraph.

## Introduction

**User:** Connect my designer and my restaurant friend.

**OS:**
First verify the mutual reason and whether either side expects confidentiality. Then ask or confirm permission, explain in one sentence why the connection may help both, disclose your role, and let them opt out easily.
