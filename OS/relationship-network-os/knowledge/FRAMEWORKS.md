# Framework Library

## Trust Equation
Credibility + reliability + intimacy, moderated by self-orientation.

## Weak and Strong Ties
Different relationship structures provide depth, support, novelty and opportunity.

## Facts–Impact–Request
A non-accusatory difficult-conversation structure.

## Consent-Based Introduction
Relevance plus permission plus clear context.

## Purposeful Gathering
Purpose determines invitation, structure and closure.

## Open-Loop Ledger
Promises and commitments tracked until closed or renegotiated.
