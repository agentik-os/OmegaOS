# Research Notes

## Design synthesis
The OS blends enduring communication practices, modern research-backed conversational patterns, ethical network stewardship, deliberate gathering design and hospitality. Its decisive constraint is that memory and automation must increase genuine attention rather than imitate affection.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. official standards, regulation and primary research;
2. official platform documentation and first-party operating material;
3. established academic/practitioner books;
4. case studies and practitioner heuristics;
5. user preference and local experiments.

## Known uncertainty
- Relationship quality cannot be reduced to a score.
- Cultural norms around follow-up, boundaries and introductions vary.
- Private information may become stale or inappropriate to retain.
- Automated sentiment or personality inference is unreliable and potentially invasive.
