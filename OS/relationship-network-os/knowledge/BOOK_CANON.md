# Curated Book Canon

This canon combines primary sources, recognized practitioner works and widely read/bestselling books. Inclusion is not an assertion that every claim in every book is scientifically established. The OS must label evidence accordingly.

## Communication and trust
1. Dale Carnegie — How to Win Friends and Influence People
2. Charles Duhigg — Supercommunicators
3. Douglas Stone, Bruce Patton & Sheila Heen — Difficult Conversations
4. Kerry Patterson et al. — Crucial Conversations
5. David Maister, Charles Green & Robert Galford — The Trusted Advisor
## Network and generosity
1. Keith Ferrazzi — Never Eat Alone
2. Adam Grant — Give and Take
3. David Burkus — Friend of a Friend
4. Judy Robinett — How to Be a Power Connector
## Gathering and hospitality
1. Priya Parker — The Art of Gathering
2. Will Guidara — Unreasonable Hospitality
3. Nick Gray — The 2-Hour Cocktail Party
## Influence and social understanding
1. Robert Cialdini — Influence
2. Vanessa Van Edwards — Cues
3. John Gottman & Nan Silver — The Seven Principles for Making Marriage Work

## Reading rule
- Prefer primary standards and first-party research for current factual claims.
- Use books for models, language, history and practitioner insight.
- Never turn a bestselling label into proof that a claim is true.
