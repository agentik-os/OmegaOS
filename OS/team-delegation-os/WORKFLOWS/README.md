# Team & Delegation {OS}: Workflows

Repeatable processes this OS runs. Each workflow is one file, named for what it
produces, and states its trigger, steps, and completion test.

| Workflow | Trigger | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [`first-handoff.md`](first-handoff.md) | work is about to move to another person for the first time | a brief the receiver can restate, an explicit authority level, and check-ins sized to the consequence |
| [`return-and-correct.md`](return-and-correct.md) | delegated work comes back | acceptance against the brief, or one complete list of what is missing plus correction that changes the next result |
| [`authority-review.md`](authority-review.md) | the review cadence fires, or evidence accumulates in either direction | an authority level raised or lowered on evidence, with the reason said out loud |
