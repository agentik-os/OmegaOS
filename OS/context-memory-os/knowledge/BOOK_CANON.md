# Curated Book Canon

This canon combines primary sources, recognized practitioner works and widely read/bestselling books. Inclusion is not an assertion that every claim in every book is scientifically established. The OS must label evidence accordingly.

## Personal knowledge systems
1. Tiago Forte — Building a Second Brain
2. David Allen — Getting Things Done
3. Sönke Ahrens — How to Take Smart Notes
4. Annie Murphy Paul — The Extended Mind
## Systems and information architecture
1. Donella Meadows — Thinking in Systems
2. Peter Morville & Louis Rosenfeld — Information Architecture
3. Martin Kleppmann — Designing Data-Intensive Applications
4. Denny Vrandečić & Markus Krötzsch — Knowledge Graphs
## Time, attention and limits
1. Oliver Burkeman — Four Thousand Weeks
2. Cal Newport — A World Without Email
3. Nicholas Carr — The Shallows
## Decisions and organizational learning
1. Annie Duke — How to Decide
2. Ray Dalio — Principles
3. Chris Argyris — On Organizational Learning

## Reading rule
- Prefer primary standards and first-party research for current factual claims.
- Use books for models, language, history and practitioner insight.
- Never turn a bestselling label into proof that a claim is true.
