# Research Notes

## Design synthesis
The architecture combines trusted capture methods from GTD and second-brain practice with provenance, event history, graph relationships, temporal validity and privacy governance. Its key departure from ordinary note apps is strict differentiation between source, extraction, inference and canonical memory.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. official standards, regulation and primary research;
2. official platform documentation and first-party operating material;
3. established academic/practitioner books;
4. case studies and practitioner heuristics;
5. user preference and local experiments.

## Known uncertainty
- Semantic retrieval can surface plausible but irrelevant records.
- Entity resolution across aliases can create damaging merges.
- Legal retention and erasure obligations vary by jurisdiction and purpose.
- Summarization can distort nuance unless source links remain available.
