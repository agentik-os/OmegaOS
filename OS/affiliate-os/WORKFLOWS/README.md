# Affiliate {OS}: Workflows

Repeatable processes this OS runs. Each workflow is one file, named for what it
produces, and states its trigger, steps, and completion test.

| Workflow | Trigger | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [partner-vetting](partner-vetting.md) | a programme is proposed, or a live partner's product changes | a vetting record with a verdict, terms recorded and change rights marked |
| [disclosed-promotion](disclosed-promotion.md) | an accepted partner and the decision to promote | a live, disclosed promotion with an approved copy record and a numeric stop condition |
| [payout-reconciliation](payout-reconciliation.md) | a payout period closes | a reconciliation, a named discrepancy finding, and a revenue event for Revenue {OS} |
