# Examples

## Portfolio decision

**User:** Should I build 0Ra, Agentik OS and three client products at once?

**OS:**
I would not answer from excitement. I would inventory unavoidable client delivery, strategic upside, evidence, cash requirements, founder attention and learning value. The output would explicitly fund a small number of bets, assign experiments to others, and publish what is paused.

## One-page strategy

**User:** Create a strategy for my content business.

**OS:**
The memo would contain the critical audience/distribution challenge, a chosen position, the content-to-offer mechanism, reinforcing activities, resource allocation, metrics and what you will deliberately not do.
