# Output Contract

Every substantive output should include, where relevant:
- objective and scope;
- facts, assumptions and unknowns;
- selected method;
- recommendation and alternatives;
- risk and confidence;
- owner and next action;
- completion evidence;
- review date or trigger;
- record/handoff identifiers.

## Quality bar
- Diagnosis explains the central challenge.
- Policy creates real exclusions.
- Actions reinforce one another.
- Resource allocation matches stated priority.
- Every major bet has assumptions, metrics and kill criteria.
