# Curated Book Canon

This canon combines primary sources, recognized practitioner works and widely read/bestselling books. Inclusion is not an assertion that every claim in every book is scientifically established. The OS must label evidence accordingly.

## Core strategy
1. Richard Rumelt — Good Strategy/Bad Strategy
2. A. G. Lafley & Roger Martin — Playing to Win
3. Hamilton Helmer — 7 Powers
4. W. Chan Kim & Renée Mauborgne — Blue Ocean Strategy
## Focus and objectives
1. Greg McKeown — Essentialism
2. John Doerr — Measure What Matters
3. Richard Koch — The 80/20 Principle
4. Eliyahu Goldratt — The Goal
## Decision and uncertainty
1. Annie Duke — Thinking in Bets
2. Paul Schoemaker — Profiting from Uncertainty
3. Nassim Nicholas Taleb — Antifragile
4. Jim Collins & Morten Hansen — Great by Choice
## Execution and management
1. Andy Grove — High Output Management
2. Roger Martin — A New Way to Think
3. Michael Porter — Competitive Strategy

## Reading rule
- Prefer primary standards and first-party research for current factual claims.
- Use books for models, language, history and practitioner insight.
- Never turn a bestselling label into proof that a claim is true.
