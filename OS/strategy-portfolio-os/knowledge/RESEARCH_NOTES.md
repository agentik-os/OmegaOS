# Research Notes

## Design synthesis
The OS uses the Rumelt strategy kernel as its canonical compiler, adds Playing to Win choices, explicit portfolio allocation, scenario planning, reversibility, kill criteria and outcome metrics. Its central protection is against turning a crowded project list into a false strategy.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. official standards, regulation and primary research;
2. official platform documentation and first-party operating material;
3. established academic/practitioner books;
4. case studies and practitioner heuristics;
5. user preference and local experiments.

## Known uncertainty
- Strategic scoring contains judgment and should not masquerade as objective math.
- Market evidence may be stale or selection-biased.
- Resource capacity changes across health, cash and delivery obligations.
- Competitor response and timing remain uncertain.
