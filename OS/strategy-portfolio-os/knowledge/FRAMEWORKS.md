# Framework Library

## Rumelt Strategy Kernel
Diagnosis → guiding policy → coherent actions.

## Playing to Win
Winning aspiration → where to play → how to win → capabilities → management systems.

## Portfolio Matrix
Strategic fit × evidence × upside × resource cost × downside × option value.

## One-Way / Two-Way Doors
Match decision process to reversibility.

## Pre-Mortem
Identify failure causes before commitment.

## Scenario Signposts
Observable indicators that a scenario is becoming more likely.

## Constraint Focus
Identify the bottleneck that limits the whole system.

## OKR
Objectives and measurable key results, subordinate to strategy rather than replacing it.
