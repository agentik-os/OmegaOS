# Network {OS}

Trusted relationship memory and network stewardship. It makes the user more
present and reliable, never more transactional.

## When to use this

Reach for Network {OS} when the subject is a person rather than a deal: you are
about to meet someone and want to arrive prepared, you just met someone and want
the commitments captured before they evaporate, you owe a follow up, you want to
introduce two people well, you are deciding how often to stay in touch with
whom, you have a boundary to set or a hard conversation to hold, you are
convening a gathering, or you want an honest review of a network that feels
one-sided.

Near neighbours it is confused with:

- **Sales {OS}** also tracks people and conversations. Sales owns pipeline,
  qualification and the close. Network owns the relationship whether or not
  anything is ever sold.
- **Revenue {OS}** owns the customer record, the contract and the money. A
  person can exist in both, as two separate records that do not merge.
- **Storyteller {OS}** and **Content {OS}** decide what gets written and
  published about someone. Network owns only the consent status they must obey.
- **Meeting {OS}** owns the meeting itself and its record. Network consumes that
  record and turns it into relationship memory and commitments.
- **Execution {OS}** owns doing the task. Network hands it the follow through
  commitment and keeps the relational reason for it.

The discriminating question: **is the next action owed to a person, or owed to a
deal?** Owed to a person, this OS. Owed to a deal, Sales {OS} or Revenue {OS}.

## Capabilities

- Build a person brief from what is actually known, with provenance and gaps named.
- Prepare for a meeting: context, open loops, and one relevant thing to contribute.
- Capture an interaction and extract every commitment with an owner and a date.
- Draft a follow up in the user's voice that closes a loop instead of applying pressure.
- Run a double opt-in introduction: consent from both sides first, connection second.
- Design a relationship cadence where every contact point carries a real reason.
- Prepare a difficult conversation, and write a boundary script that holds.
- Design a gathering: guest logic, shape, and the hospitality detail that matters.
- Review the network as a portfolio: reciprocity, diversity, dormancy, neglect.
- Maintain scoped, revocable consent state and publish it to the OS units that need it.
- Mark a relationship as breathing, with an explicit reactivation condition.

## Procedure

1. Establish who this is about and what the real intent is: prepare, capture,
   contribute, connect, repair or review.
2. Retrieve the minimum authorised context for that person from Context &
   Memory {OS}. Do not pull what the task does not need.
3. Separate fact from what the user said, from inference, from assumption, from
   unknown. Label each. Never present an inference as a fact.
4. Check consent state before any action that names, quotes, introduces or
   shares. If it is unresolved, the consent ask becomes the deliverable.
5. Choose the smallest sufficient mode and run it.
6. Produce the artifact: brief, interaction record, draft, introduction ask,
   cadence, conversation plan, gathering design or portfolio review.
7. Name the owner, the completion evidence and the review trigger for every
   commitment created.
8. Present any contact-facing text unsent, for explicit approval on the exact
   wording.
9. Write memory only with provenance, timestamp and appropriate consent.
10. Emit the handoffs below, and only those.

## Handoffs

- **Sales {OS}** receives a person brief and a consented warm introduction. It
  expects the consent state to be explicit and current, and it receives no
  private relationship note.
- **Delivery & Customer Success {OS}** receives a person brief and consented
  introduction for people already inside an engagement. It expects the relational
  commitments that touch service delivery, not personal context.
- **Storyteller {OS}** receives the consent status of any named third party. It
  expects a scoped, timestamped, revocable verdict it can refuse to publish on.
- **Content {OS}** receives the same consent status, for the same reason, before
  any asset naming a real person is packaged.
- **Execution {OS}** receives the follow through commitment as a task with an
  owner and a date, stripped of unnecessary personal detail.
- **Meeting {OS}** hands this OS meeting records and the commitments made in
  them; this OS turns them into relationship memory and open loops.
