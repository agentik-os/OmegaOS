# Research {OS}: Workflows

Repeatable processes this OS runs. Each workflow is one file, named for what it
produces, and states its trigger, steps, and completion test.

| Workflow | Trigger | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [research-memo.md](research-memo.md) | a question needs answering and the corpus does not already hold the answer | the memo: every claim sourced, dated and classed, contradictions and unknowns visible, confidence stated with its reason |
| [source-vetting-pass.md](source-vetting-pass.md) | sources are in hand and someone is about to quote them | the vetted source ledger: tier, interest, weakness and access outcome per source, including everything blocked |
| [verified-claim.md](verified-claim.md) | one claim is load-bearing, or a number is circulating with no known origin | the claim with its provenance chain walked back to the primary, classed, dated and marked with its support type |
| [contradiction-report.md](contradiction-report.md) | credible sources disagree on the same sub-question | the contradiction record: both sides with their evidence, the cause of the disagreement, and what would settle it |
