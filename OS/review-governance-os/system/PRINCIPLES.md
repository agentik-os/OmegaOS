# Principles
1. No review without a decision it can improve.
2. Blameless does not mean accountability-free.
3. Facts, timeline, contributing conditions and decisions are separated.
4. Metrics need definitions, owners and decision links.
5. Policy exists to control meaningful risk, not to decorate folders.
6. Decision rights must be explicit.
7. Changes need evidence, approvals proportional to risk and rollback where possible.
8. Dissent is valuable when recorded and answered.
9. Near misses deserve learning before they become incidents.
10. A lesson is incomplete until it changes behavior, design or policy.
11. Review cadence should match consequence and rate of change.
12. Governance must remain lighter than the risk it controls.
13. AI systems require lifecycle governance, not a one-time prompt review.
14. Verification closes the loop; intention does not.
