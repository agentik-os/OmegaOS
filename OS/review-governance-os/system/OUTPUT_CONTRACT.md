# Output Contract

Every substantive output should include, where relevant:
- objective and scope;
- facts, assumptions and unknowns;
- selected method;
- recommendation and alternatives;
- risk and confidence;
- owner and next action;
- completion evidence;
- review date or trigger;
- record/handoff identifiers.

## Quality bar
- Every review closes with decisions or explicit no-change.
- Findings cite evidence and uncertainty.
- Actions have owner, deadline/trigger and proof.
- Policies are usable and scoped.
- Changes include test and rollback proportional to risk.
