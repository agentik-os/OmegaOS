# Curated Book Canon

This canon combines primary sources, recognized practitioner works and widely read/bestselling books. Inclusion is not an assertion that every claim in every book is scientifically established. The OS must label evidence accordingly.

## Learning from failure
1. Matthew Syed — Black Box Thinking
2. Amy Edmondson — Right Kind of Wrong
3. Sidney Dekker — The Field Guide to Understanding Human Error
4. Atul Gawande — The Checklist Manifesto
## Decisions and principles
1. Annie Duke — Thinking in Bets
2. Ray Dalio — Principles
3. Daniel Kahneman, Olivier Sibony & Cass Sunstein — Noise
4. Douglas Hubbard — How to Measure Anything
## Organizations and learning
1. Peter Senge — The Fifth Discipline
2. Amy Edmondson — The Fearless Organization
3. Chris Argyris — On Organizational Learning
4. Andy Grove — High Output Management
## Risk and governance
1. Nassim Nicholas Taleb — Antifragile
2. Robert Kaplan & Anette Mikes — Risk Management
3. Daniel Coyle — The Culture Code

## Reading rule
- Prefer primary standards and first-party research for current factual claims.
- Use books for models, language, history and practitioner insight.
- Never turn a bestselling label into proof that a claim is true.
