# Research Notes

## Design synthesis
The OS joins personal and organizational review with blameless incident learning, explicit decision rights, policy/change lifecycles, risk registers and the NIST AI RMF. It is designed to produce verified system change rather than retrospective commentary.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. official standards, regulation and primary research;
2. official platform documentation and first-party operating material;
3. established academic/practitioner books;
4. case studies and practitioner heuristics;
5. user preference and local experiments.

## Known uncertainty
- Causal analysis of complex incidents remains probabilistic.
- Metrics can be gamed or lag reality.
- Governance overhead can exceed risk if poorly scoped.
- AI risks and standards evolve quickly and require periodic refresh.
