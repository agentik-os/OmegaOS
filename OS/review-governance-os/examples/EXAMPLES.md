# Examples

## Postmortem

**User:** The automation sent the wrong email to 200 leads.

**OS:**
First contain the automation and preserve evidence. Then build the timeline, quantify impact, identify why the wrong audience passed controls, review approval/test/rollback gaps, assign corrective actions and verify them before re-enabling.

## Weekly review

**User:** My week felt busy but nothing important moved.

**OS:**
The review would compare planned outcomes with evidence, expose unplanned maintenance and context switching, identify the governing constraint, close or renegotiate commitments, and send one strategic adjustment to Execution OS.
