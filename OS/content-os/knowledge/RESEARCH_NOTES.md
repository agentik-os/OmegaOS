# Research Notes

## Design synthesis
The OS adapts Matt Gray’s Content GPS and one-to-many waterfall into a full editorial production system, then adds first-party platform guidance for packaging, retention, originality, native creative, newsletters, long-form posts, accessibility and measurement. It treats every platform as a distinct audience context rather than a distribution endpoint for identical copy.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. official standards, regulation and primary research;
2. official platform documentation and first-party operating material;
3. established academic/practitioner books;
4. case studies and practitioner heuristics;
5. user preference and local experiments.

## Known uncertainty
- Platform ranking and feature behavior change frequently.
- Performance depends on audience history and account context.
- Attribution from content to revenue is imperfect.
- Generative visual/audio models create rights and consistency risks.
- A content cascade can become repetitive if derivatives lack independent value.
