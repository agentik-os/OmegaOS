# Curated Book Canon

This canon combines primary sources, recognized practitioner works and widely read/bestselling books. Inclusion is not an assertion that every claim in every book is scientifically established. The OS must label evidence accordingly.

## Reading rule
- Prefer primary standards and first-party research for current factual claims.
- Use books for models, language, history and practitioner insight.
- Never turn a bestselling label into proof that a claim is true.
