# Protocol — Content Waterfall

Dissect one pillar into a configurable cascade: long video/article/newsletter, short clips/Reels/Shorts/TikToks, LinkedIn documents/posts, X posts/thread/Article, Instagram carousel, quote/visual assets and community prompts. Every derivative must stand alone and be native.

## Required closure
- decision or output
- owner
- observable completion evidence
- review trigger
- memory/handoff instruction
