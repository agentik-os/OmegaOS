# YouTube Long-Form Playbook

## Audience context
A mix of active intent (search) and recommendation-driven curiosity. The title/thumbnail earns the click; sustained fulfillment earns retention and satisfaction.

## Package-first workflow
1. Define viewer, problem and transformation.
2. Generate title–thumbnail concept pairs before full production.
3. Ensure the package is accurate rather than clickbait.
4. Write the cold open to confirm the promise in the first seconds.
5. Structure escalating value, examples, proof, pattern interrupts and a complete payoff.

## Required package
Topic thesis; viewer promise; 5–10 title options; 3 thumbnail concepts; cold open; full outline/script; retention map; shot/B-roll/graphics; sound; chapters; description; citations; end-screen/CTA; Shorts cascade; test plan.

## Quality questions
Would the correct viewer click? Is the promise fulfilled quickly? Does each section justify continued attention? Is there a reason to trust the claim? Does the ending deliver closure and next step?

## Metrics
Impressions/CTR interpreted with source, first-30-second retention, retention curve, average view duration/percentage, returning viewers, satisfaction signals, comments and downstream action.

## Failure patterns
Packaging created after production, title/thumbnail mismatch, long biography intro, repeated points, unsupported claims, forced retention gimmicks.
