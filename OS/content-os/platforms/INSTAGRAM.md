# Instagram Playbook

## Audience context
Visual discovery plus relationship maintenance. Users often have little context in Explore/Reels but more context in Stories and DMs.

## Jobs by format
- **Reels:** discovery, demonstration, personality, rapid lessons.
- **Carousels:** structured education, frameworks, proof, saves and shares.
- **Photos:** identity, taste, place, product and human credibility.
- **Stories:** continuity, behind-the-scenes, polls, conversation and conversion.

## Creative grammar
1. First frame communicates topic/tension without audio dependency.
2. Original source material and a recognizable point of view.
3. Vertical-safe composition for Reels; readable type and restrained slide density for carousels.
4. Captions add context rather than restating every visual.
5. One primary interaction: watch, save, share, reply, visit or apply.

## Required output package
Hook options; script/slide copy; caption; cover; shot/design brief; on-screen text; subtitle/alt-text plan; CTA; rights status; experiment hypothesis.

## Metrics to interpret
Reach/discovery, hold and completion, saves, shares, profile actions, replies/DMs and qualified downstream intent. Do not optimize one metric in isolation.

## Failure patterns
Copied trend without relevance; generic list carousel; unreadable text; aesthetic without idea; private-person story without consent; every post acting as a sales ad.
