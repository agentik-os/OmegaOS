# X Playbook

## Audience context
Fast ideas, current conversation, intellectual positioning and relationship through replies.

## Formats
- Single post: one quotable complete idea.
- Thread: a claim that needs progression, proof or steps.
- Reply: specific contribution to another person's context.
- Article: long-form argument with media/embeds when available.

## Rules
Lead with the idea, not throat-clearing. Prefer concrete language. Use hashtags sparingly—X's own business guidance recommends no more than two when useful. Add image descriptions/alt text. Separate timely reaction from durable thesis.

## Required package
Core claim; 5 openings; single post/thread/Article version; evidence links; image/video brief; reply prompts; CTA; accessibility.

## Failure patterns
Empty aphorisms, copied threads, contextless controversy, excessive hashtags, fake certainty, scheduling without participating in replies.
