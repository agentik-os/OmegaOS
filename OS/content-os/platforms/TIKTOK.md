# TikTok Playbook

## Audience context
Low-context, entertainment-first discovery. Credibility is earned inside the clip, not assumed from title or follower count.

## Creative grammar
- Begin with situation, visual change, tension, demonstration or strong claim immediately.
- Use a TikTok-first vertical language: human delivery, native pacing, captions and culturally legible editing.
- Treat trends as storytelling templates, not mandatory costumes.
- Show process, proof and personality; remove corporate preamble.
- Refresh creative and test distinct openings rather than micro-editing one weak idea forever.

## Asset types
Direct-to-camera lesson; demonstration; day-in-the-life scene; reaction with original analysis; story; before/after with evidence; series; reply-to-comment.

## Required package
First-frame concepts; 3 hook variants; spoken script/beat sheet; visual actions; captions; edit and sound brief; comment prompt; rights/accessibility; test plan.

## Metrics
Initial hold, average watch time, completion/rewatch, shares, comments, profile actions and qualified intent. Interpret by video length and audience source.

## Failure patterns
Horizontal/corporate creative, slow introductions, unexplained context, trend chasing without fit, reused watermarked assets, misleading payoff.
