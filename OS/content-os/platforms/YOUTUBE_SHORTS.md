# YouTube Shorts Playbook

A Short must be complete for a viewer who has never seen the pillar. Open with immediate context, keep one idea, create visual progression and deliver a satisfying payoff. A cut from long-form is approved only when it stands alone.

**Package:** first frame, 1-sentence promise, 15–60 second beat sheet as appropriate, captions, visual changes, sound, title/description, related-video bridge, retention hypothesis.

**Avoid:** “watch the full video” before giving value, cropped horizontal footage with tiny subject, missing context, excessive setup, identical reposts across every short platform.
