# Newsletter Playbook

## Audience context
Permission-based, owned attention with higher expectation of continuity and depth.

## Edition architecture
1. One clear reader promise in subject/preheader.
2. Opening that confirms relevance.
3. Story, argument or practical framework.
4. Evidence/examples and a useful takeaway.
5. One primary CTA; secondary links remain visibly secondary.
6. Reply invitation when conversation is valuable.

## System
Welcome sequence; segmentation by interest/intent; recurring editorial promise; campaign vs broadcast distinction; deliverability and consent; archive/evergreen reuse.

## Metrics
Delivery, opens used cautiously, clicks, replies, conversions, unsubscribes, spam complaints and cohort retention.

## Failure patterns
Multiple competing CTAs, clickbait subject mismatch, sending every platform post by email, no segmentation, hidden sponsorship or weak unsubscribe practices.
