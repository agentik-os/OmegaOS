# LinkedIn Playbook

## Audience context
Professional identity, competence, business relevance and network conversation. Readers reward concrete insight, first-hand experience, useful frameworks and credible proof.

## Formats and jobs
- Text post: one focused insight or decision.
- Document/carousel: framework, playbook, case or visual explanation.
- Native video: concise professional lesson, demonstration or point of view; front-load message and caption for muted viewing.
- Article/newsletter: deeper recurring authority and owned subscriber relationship inside LinkedIn.

## Structure
Professional relevance → specific tension/claim → evidence/example → practical implication → genuine question or next action.

## Required package
Audience/role, business problem, insight, proof, post/document/video copy, visual pages or shot plan, alt text/captions, CTA, comment response plan.

## Failure patterns
Generic inspiration, false vulnerability, engagement bait, unverified business numbers, excessive jargon, content with no connection to work decisions.
