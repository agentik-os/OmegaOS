# Podcast & Audio Playbook

Define the listener promise, emotional/intellectual journey and why audio is the right medium. Prepare an arc rather than a rigid interrogation. Capture clean source audio; design intro/outro, music and effects with rights; edit for clarity while preserving humanity; provide chapters, show notes and transcript; extract clips that stand alone.

Metrics: starts, consumption, completion, follower/subscriber growth, replies, clip-to-episode movement and qualitative listener evidence.
