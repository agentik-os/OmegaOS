# Platform Strategy Matrix

Research snapshot: 2026-08-11. Revalidate first-party platform guidance before major campaigns.

| Platform | Typical audience state | Best strategic jobs | Native strengths | Primary quality questions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | visual browsing, identity, discovery, social proof | discovery, aspiration, education, relationship | Reels, carousels, photos, Stories, DMs | Is it original, visually coherent, immediately understandable and worth saving/sharing? |
| TikTok | entertainment-first discovery, low prior context | rapid discovery, cultural relevance, demonstration | vertical video, native editing, trends/templates, comments | Does it feel made for TikTok, establish context instantly and earn continued viewing? |
| YouTube Long | active curiosity/search plus recommendation | depth, trust, education, evergreen authority | title/thumbnail packaging, long narrative, chapters | Does the package make an accurate promise and does the video continually fulfill it? |
| YouTube Shorts | fast vertical discovery | reach, idea tests, moments, bridge to depth | vertical standalone clips | Does it work without prior context and deliver a complete payoff? |
| LinkedIn | professional identity and work relevance | authority, proof, cases, hiring, B2B demand | insight posts, documents, video, articles/newsletters | Is the insight professionally useful, specific and supported by experience/proof? |
| X | real-time ideas, debate, networked conversation | intellectual positioning, feedback, relationships, news reaction | concise posts, threads, replies, Articles | Is the idea sharp enough to quote, discuss or build upon? |
| Newsletter | opted-in owned attention | depth, trust, conversion, continuity | segmentation, long form, direct reply | Is the subject promise clear, the body valuable and the next action singular? |
| Blog / SEO | explicit information need | durable discovery, evidence, product education | search intent, structured articles, internal linking | Does the page fully satisfy the query and show trustworthy first-hand/primary evidence? |
| Podcast | intimate, time-rich listening | depth, personality, network and long-form conversation | voice, dialogue, seriality, clips | Is there a clear listener promise, strong conversation arc and clean audio? |
| Reddit / community | context-specific peer exchange | research, credibility, support, discussion | detailed posts, comments, community norms | Does it contribute before promoting and respect the community's local rules? |

## Cascade rule
The pillar supplies truth and depth. The platform playbook determines opening, structure, duration, visual grammar, CTA and interaction. No asset is approved because it was merely resized.
