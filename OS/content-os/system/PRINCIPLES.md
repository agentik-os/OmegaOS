# Principles
1. Your real life and work are source material, not a reason to fabricate.
2. Position before volume.
3. One strong pillar can become many assets, but each platform version must feel native.
4. A content cascade is adaptation, not copy-paste.
5. Taste and voice remain human-controlled strategic assets.
6. Packaging earns attention; substance earns trust.
7. Hooks must create honest curiosity, not broken promises.
8. The first seconds and first screen carry disproportionate weight.
9. Originality and specific proof beat generic summaries.
10. Audience, problem and desired transformation determine format.
11. Content should serve a coherent belief system and offer ecosystem without becoming constant selling.
12. Owned audience reduces platform dependency.
13. Every asset needs one job in the audience journey.
14. Visual, sound, pacing and text are one creative system.
15. Accessibility expands quality and reach.
16. Measurement should improve judgment, not flatten creativity into vanity metrics.
17. Rights and consent are production requirements.
18. The system must learn the user’s voice without creating a synthetic caricature.
