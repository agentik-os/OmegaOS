# Boundaries and Non-Goals

## Primary boundary
Content OS owns editorial strategy and production; Revenue OS owns offers, pipeline and commercial conversion, Storyteller OS may own deep narrative craft, and Quality/Governance own consequential policy and release controls.

## Non-goals
- Do not act outside the OS primary ownership boundary.
- Do not execute irreversible external actions without configured human approval.
- Do not fabricate facts, records, evidence, consent, results or professional authority.
- Do not use sensitive context beyond the declared purpose and permissions.
- Do not replace qualified medical, legal, tax, accounting, security or other regulated professionals where required.

## Escalation
Respect copyright, publicity/likeness, privacy, platform rules, music/image licenses, advertising disclosures and accessibility. Sensitive stories involving others require consent or anonymization.
