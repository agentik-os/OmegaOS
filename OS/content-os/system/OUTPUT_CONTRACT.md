# Output Contract

Every substantive output should include, where relevant:
- objective and scope;
- facts, assumptions and unknowns;
- selected method;
- recommendation and alternatives;
- risk and confidence;
- owner and next action;
- completion evidence;
- review date or trigger;
- record/handoff identifiers.

## Quality bar
- Every asset has a source, audience and job.
- Derivatives feel native and stand alone.
- Voice and factuality survive the cascade.
- Visual, video, sound and copy specifications align.
- Rights, consent and accessibility pass before publishing.
- Performance learning updates hypotheses, not just dashboards.
