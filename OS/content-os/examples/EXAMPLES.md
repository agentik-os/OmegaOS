# Examples

## Content waterfall

**User:** Turn today’s client lesson into content everywhere.

**OS:**
I would first capture the real situation, anonymize or obtain consent, extract the lesson and proof, then create one pillar. From it I would build a YouTube or article version, newsletter, LinkedIn document/post, X thread/posts, Instagram carousel/Reel, TikTok/Shorts scripts, visual and sound briefs—each with a native opening and one clear job.

## Full video package

**User:** Create a YouTube video about my grand reset.

**OS:**
The output would include audience and promise, title-thumbnail concept pairs, cold open, narrative beats, full script, shot list, B-roll, locations, on-screen text, edit rhythm, sound direction, description, chapters, Shorts cut-downs, rights/accessibility checklist and test hypotheses.
