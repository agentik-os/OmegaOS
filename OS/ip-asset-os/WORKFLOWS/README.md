# IP & Asset {OS}: Workflows

Repeatable processes this OS runs. Each workflow is one file, named for what it
produces, and states its trigger, steps, and completion test.

| Workflow | Trigger | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [IP inventory and gap scan](ip-inventory-and-gap-scan.md) | first run, a diligence request, a new contributor, or quarterly | the asset register, and the ranked list of assets whose ownership cannot be proven |
| [Licence grant review](license-grant-review.md) | a grant out or in is proposed, agreed, signed, or up for renewal | a licence record with seven terms, an exclusivity conflict check, and the counsel brief |
| [Renewal and deadline sweep](renewal-deadline-sweep.md) | monthly, before an absence, or when a registration or licence is recorded | dated obligations with verified dates, lead times, named human owners and the professional to instruct |

The inventory workflow runs before the other two. A licence granted on an asset
whose title is unproven, and a renewal calendar built over an incomplete
register, are both worse than nothing: they read as coverage where there is
none.
