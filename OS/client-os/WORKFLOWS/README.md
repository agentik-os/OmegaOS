# Client {OS}: Workflows

Repeatable processes this OS runs. Each workflow is one file, named for what it
produces, and states its trigger, steps, and completion test.

| Workflow | Trigger | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [`engagement-open.md`](engagement-open.md) | an engagement is agreed and work is about to start | an expectation record confirmed in writing by the client, and the account brief anyone can pick up |
| [`bad-news-early.md`](bad-news-early.md) | a slip, a failure or a miss becomes known internally | a message sent the day it is known, carrying the recovery option and what changes for the client |
| [`boundary-answer.md`](boundary-answer.md) | the client asks for something outside what was agreed | one of three answers, included, extra with a price, or refused with an alternative, plus the change or exception record |
| [`account-health.md`](account-health.md) | the review cadence fires, or a strain signal appears | a health read with evidence, and a named repair with an owner and a date for anything not green |
