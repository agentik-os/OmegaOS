# Workflow: bad news, early

Produces a message that reaches the client the day the bad news is known, with
the recovery already chosen.

## Trigger

A slip, a failure, a defect, a miss or a resourcing loss becomes known
internally. The trigger is the moment it is known, not the moment it lands.

## Inputs

- The fact, from Project {OS}, Delivery & CS {OS} or Execution {OS}.
- The recovery option set and the option that has been chosen internally.
- The expectation record: what the client was promised and when.
- The relationship history, including any previous bad news.

## Steps

1. **Confirm the fact internally first.** Bad news that turns out to be wrong
   costs more credibility than the original problem.
2. **Choose the recovery before writing.** Never send a problem without a
   proposal; that transfers the work of solving it to the client.
3. **Write four parts, in this order:** what happened, what it means for them,
   what is being done and by when, and what you need from them, if anything.
4. **Lead with the fact.** No warm-up paragraph. The client will find the fact
   anyway, and burying it reads as an attempt to hide it.
5. **Give one date, and make it real.** A date backed by capacity that already
   exists. A second slip on the same item is far more expensive than the first.
6. **Do not over-apologise.** One clear acknowledgement, then the plan. Apology
   theatre reads as a substitute for a fix.
7. **Say what changed structurally** if this is the second occurrence of the
   same failure. The client is entitled to know it will not happen a third time.
8. **Get human approval and send.** Same day. Client {OS} drafts; the human
   sends.
9. **Record it** in the client ledger and in the promise ledger, and feed the
   cause into the weekly reset and, when systemic, into Review & Governance {OS}.

## Completion test

- The message went out on the day the news was known internally.
- It contains the fact, the consequence for the client, the recovery with a
  date, and any ask.
- The date is backed by capacity that exists in the plan.
- It was approved by a human before sending.
- The event is recorded in the client ledger, with the cause routed onward.

## Failure paths

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| the team wants to wait for certainty | send what is known, name the uncertainty, and give the date on which the uncertainty resolves |
| the recovery is not yet chosen | run the recovery workflow in Project {OS} first, same day, then send |
| the client reacts badly | that is a strain signal; diagnose the cause and run the repair, do not concede scope in the moment |
| the same news has been sent before | say what has structurally changed, and if nothing has, say that too and escalate internally |
