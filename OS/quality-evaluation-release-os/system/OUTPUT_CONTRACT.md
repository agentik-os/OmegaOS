# Output Contract

Every substantive output should include, where relevant:
- objective and scope;
- facts, assumptions and unknowns;
- selected method;
- recommendation and alternatives;
- risk and confidence;
- owner and next action;
- completion evidence;
- review date or trigger;
- record/handoff identifiers.

## Quality bar
- No critical requirement lacks evidence or accepted rationale.
- Security, privacy and accessibility are explicit gates.
- AI changes run representative and adversarial regression.
- Release pack pins every consequential version.
- Rollout, observability and rollback are tested—not aspirational.
