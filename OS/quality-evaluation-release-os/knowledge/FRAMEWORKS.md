# Framework Library

## Risk-Based Quality
Prioritize evidence by impact, likelihood, change and uncertainty.

## Traceability Matrix
Requirement ↔ test/eval ↔ result ↔ defect ↔ release.

## DORA Delivery Metrics
Deployment frequency, lead time, failed deployment recovery time, change fail rate and deployment rework rate.

## OWASP ASVS
Application-security verification requirements.

## NIST SSDF
Secure software-development practices.

## WCAG 2.2
Accessibility success criteria and conformance.

## SLSA
Supply-chain integrity and provenance levels.

## OpenTelemetry
Vendor-neutral telemetry for traces, metrics, logs and profiles.

## NIST AI RMF
Govern, Map, Measure and Manage AI risk.

## SLO / Error Budget
Reliability target and controlled risk appetite.
