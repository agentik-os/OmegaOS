# Curated Book Canon

This canon combines primary sources, recognized practitioner works and widely read/bestselling books. Inclusion is not an assertion that every claim in every book is scientifically established. The OS must label evidence accordingly.

## Delivery and quality
1. Nicole Forsgren, Jez Humble & Gene Kim — Accelerate
2. Jez Humble & David Farley — Continuous Delivery
3. Titus Winters, Tom Manshreck & Hyrum Wright — Software Engineering at Google
4. James Whittaker, Jason Arbon & Jeff Carollo — How Google Tests Software
## Reliability and operations
1. Betsy Beyer et al. — Site Reliability Engineering
2. Betsy Beyer et al. — The Site Reliability Workbook
3. Michael Nygard — Release It!
4. Heather Adkins et al. — Building Secure & Reliable Systems
## Testing and exploration
1. Elisabeth Hendrickson — Explore It!
2. Lisa Crispin & Janet Gregory — Agile Testing
3. Gerard Meszaros — xUnit Test Patterns
4. Michael Feathers — Working Effectively with Legacy Code
## Security and systems
1. Adam Shostack — Threat Modeling
2. Ross Anderson — Security Engineering
3. Casey Rosenthal et al. — Chaos Engineering
4. Matthew Skelton & Manuel Pais — Team Topologies

## Reading rule
- Prefer primary standards and first-party research for current factual claims.
- Use books for models, language, history and practitioner insight.
- Never turn a bestselling label into proof that a claim is true.
