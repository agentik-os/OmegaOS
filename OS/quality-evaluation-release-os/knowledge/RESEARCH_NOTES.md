# Research Notes

## Design synthesis
The release authority combines DORA delivery evidence, risk-based testing, OWASP ASVS/current application and GenAI risks, NIST SSDF and AI RMF, WCAG 2.2, SLSA provenance, SBOM transparency, OpenTelemetry observability and SRE recovery. It treats AI evaluation as a living regression discipline rather than one prompt demo.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. official standards, regulation and primary research;
2. official platform documentation and first-party operating material;
3. established academic/practitioner books;
4. case studies and practitioner heuristics;
5. user preference and local experiments.

## Known uncertainty
- Standards and platform threat models evolve quickly.
- Test environments cannot reproduce every production interaction.
- AI evaluations are sensitive to dataset, rubric and grader design.
- A release decision always retains residual risk.
