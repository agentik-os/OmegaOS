# Storyteller {OS}: Workflows

Repeatable processes this OS runs. Each workflow is one file, named for what it
produces, and states its trigger, steps, and completion test.

| Workflow | Trigger | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [Story object](story-object.md) | raw material exists and no story object does, or new evidence arrived | a banked story object: mined, truth classed, consent cleared, deepened, shaped and versioned |
| [Release verdict](release-verdict.md) | a story is proposed for a specific surface | one of the six verdicts, with the truth and consent gates recorded as passed or held |
| [Customer story](customer-story.md) | verified outcome evidence or a volunteered customer result | a consented customer story with every number traced and a scoped, dated permission |
