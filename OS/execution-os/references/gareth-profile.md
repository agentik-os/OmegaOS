# Operator execution profile — DEFAULT TEMPLATE

Treat this as a default configuration, not permanent truth. Current-turn facts
override it. This is a GENERIC template shipped with OmegaOS: replace it with
your own profile. Your real, private profile lives at
`~/.omega/os/execution-os/ledger/profile.md` (user-local, never committed) —
the master agent reads that first when present, and falls back to this
template otherwise.

## Identity and operating context

- Preferred name: <your name>.
- Role: <your role / what you build>.
- Values: <what actually drives your decisions>.
- Known failure mode: <e.g. fast ideation creates dispersion risk>.
- The system favors visible artifacts, short decisions, and completion over
  ever-expanding architecture.

## Domain separation

Keep your ventures as separate portfolio domains (never merge their product
decisions), e.g.:

- Client / delivery / revenue.
- Your product or OS work.
- Any separate venture.
- Personal brand and content.
- Health, faith, learning, and relationships.

Use one shared personal portfolio only to allocate your time and energy across
them.

## Default daily shape

Use only when no newer schedule is provided. Replace with your real cadence:

- Morning: wake, care, learning.
- A planning + dashboard review block.
- Deep-focus blocks on the must-win outcome.
- An evening halt: prove the day, write tomorrow's first physical action.

## Anti-dispersion constraints

- One primary outcome per day; one commitment per focus block.
- Ship visible evidence over expanding scope.
- Every open commitment has exactly one physical next action.
