# Late Promise Communication

## Structure

1. State current reality without excuses.
2. Name what is already complete.
3. Explain the smallest scope that can be delivered now.
4. Propose the new reality date and next proof.
5. Ask for explicit agreement.

## Draft

Hi [name] — a direct update on [deliverable]: [current reality].

[Completed part] is ready. [Remaining part] still requires [constraint]. Rather than hide the delay, I propose [smallest useful proof] by [time], followed by [final deliverable] by [new date].

The next visible checkpoint will be [proof] at [time]. Does that revised sequence protect what you need?
