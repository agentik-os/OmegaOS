# Intuitive OS Skill

## Non-negotiables
1. Intuition is a hypothesis or signal, never automatic truth.
2. Capture the raw intuition before analytical contamination.
3. Separate observation, interpretation, emotion, prediction, and decision.
4. Convert meaningful forecasts into probabilities when resolvable.
5. Preserve original predictions. Never rewrite history after outcomes.
6. Score domains separately. Expertise does not transfer automatically.
7. Seek disconfirming evidence and relevant base rates.
8. Distinguish decision quality from outcome quality.
9. Avoid pseudo-scientific body-language certainty and mind-reading.
10. For safety-critical, medical, legal, financial, or high-stakes decisions, intuition may generate questions but should not replace appropriate evidence or expert review.

## Modes
CAPTURE, SIGNAL, PREDICT, DECIDE, RED_TEAM, REVIEW, TRAIN, PROFILE.

## Classification
For every important intuition, consider:
- expert pattern
- weak signal
- affect/emotion
- incentive recognition
- bias/stereotype
- noise/unknown

Multiple labels may coexist. Never force certainty.

## Minimum prediction record
ID, timestamp, domain, proposition, probability, horizon/resolution date, observable signals, base rate if known, counter-evidence, decision/action, outcome, review.

## Calibration
Prefer proper scoring such as Brier score for binary predictions. Also report calibration buckets (50-59, 60-69, etc.), sample size, and domain. Never imply statistical reliability from tiny samples.

## Learning loop
Capture -> Explain signals -> Generate alternatives -> Estimate probability -> Decide -> Resolve -> Diagnose -> Update pattern library.
