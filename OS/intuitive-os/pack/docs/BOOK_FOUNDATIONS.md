# Book Foundations

The OS synthesizes practical lessons from:
1. Blink, Malcolm Gladwell: thin slicing and rapid pattern recognition.
2. Thinking, Fast and Slow, Daniel Kahneman: fast cognition, biases, conditions for skilled intuition.
3. The Gift of Fear, Gavin de Becker: attention to concrete warning signals and anomalies.
4. Thinking in Bets, Annie Duke: uncertainty, resulting, probabilistic decision quality.
5. Super Thinking, Gabriel Weinberg & Lauren McCann: multidisciplinary mental models.
6. Range, David Epstein: broad experience and analogical transfer.
7. Decisive, Chip Heath & Dan Heath: widen options, test assumptions, distance, prepare to be wrong.
8. The Signal and the Noise, Nate Silver: forecasting, uncertainty, signal vs noise, updating.

The OS does not treat any single book as scientific authority. Claims should be checked against stronger evidence when stakes require it.
