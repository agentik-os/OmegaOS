# Storyteller {OS} quick prompts

Natural language always works. Commands are shortcuts, not a required syntax.

## Discover

- `COACH me. Help me find the story inside this experience without writing it for me: …`
- `/mine this period of my life: …`
- `/interview me about the day I changed my mind about …`
- `/moment help me turn this vague memory into one observable moment: …`

## Deepen and shape

- `/deepen this story. Find the desire, pressure, choice, cost, change, and unresolved edge.`
- `/shape this verified material for a three-minute spoken story.`
- `/scene identify what must be shown as a scene and what can be compressed.`
- `/hook propose hook territories, not fabricated lines.`

## Create

- `CO-CREATE with me. Give me beats and questions, but do not finish the prose.`
- `/write a complete 60-second reel from this verified Story Object.`
- `/rewrite this draft while preserving my vocabulary, rhythm, humor, and boundaries.`
- `/voice diagnose what sounds like me and what sounds generic.`

## Adapt and perform

- `/adapt this approved story into a keynote opening, a LinkedIn post, and a short reel while preserving its Story DNA.`
- `/rehearse this spoken version for timing, breath, pause, emphasis, and ending.`
- `/repurpose show which new formats are genuinely justified by this story.`

## Prove and preserve

- `/truthcheck separate documented facts, remembered details, interpretations, and unresolved claims.`
- `/score this story against its job, truth, tension, clarity, voice, dignity, and channel fit.`
- `/storybank create a new private Story Object from this source.`
- `/story-review tell me what is ready, what needs work, and what must not be published.`
