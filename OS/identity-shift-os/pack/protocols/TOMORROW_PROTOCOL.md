# Tomorrow Protocol Generator

## Output template

MISSION
One sentence describing the day's strategic intent.

OBJECTIVE 1
- action
- success condition
- deadline or trigger

OBJECTIVE 2
- action
- success condition
- deadline or trigger

OBJECTIVE 3
Only include when realistic.

NON-NEGOTIABLES
Only previously agreed anchors.

FRICTION REMOVAL
One action that makes success easier.

RECOVERY
One action if fatigue/stress is elevated.

OPTIONAL CHALLENGE
Pick one:
- courage
- social
- curiosity
- environment
- creativity
- discipline
- generosity
- exposure

## Selection logic

1. Fix the highest-cost contradiction first.
2. Prefer outputs over activity.
3. Prefer one hard conversation over 20 low-value tasks.
4. Prefer shipping over planning when enough clarity exists.
5. Protect health and sleep.
