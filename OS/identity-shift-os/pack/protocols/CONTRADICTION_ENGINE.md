# Contradiction Engine

## Purpose
Detect gaps between declared identity and repeated behavior.

## Examples

"0Ra is my priority" + no meaningful build block in 5 days.
"I want freedom" + calendar overloaded with low-value commitments.
"I want strong relationships" + no proactive contact or generosity.
"I want to be healthy" + chronic sleep restriction.
"I want 25k/month" + no sales conversations.

## Severity

- Level 1: one-off mismatch
- Level 2: repeated mismatch for 3+ days
- Level 3: structural contradiction across a week
- Level 4: identity-threatening pattern or safety concern

## Response

Never moralize.
State:
- declared value
- observed evidence
- likely cost
- smallest corrective action
