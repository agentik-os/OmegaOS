# Market Research {OS}: Workflows

Repeatable processes this OS runs. Each workflow is one file, named for what it
produces, and states its trigger, steps, and completion test.

| Workflow | Trigger | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [market-decision-pack.md](market-decision-pack.md) | a launch, funding or build decision is pending, or a concept arrives on `brainstorm.concept.selected` | the versioned evidence body and one bounded decision with conditions, kill criteria and expiry, plus the Blueprint Input Manifest when eligible |
| [market-sizing-model.md](market-sizing-model.md) | a market size is needed, or a circulating figure has no traceable inputs | an auditable TAM, SAM and SOM from named inputs, with ranges, sensitivity and independent estimation paths compared rather than averaged |
| [competitive-map.md](competitive-map.md) | a concept must be placed against what buyers use today, or a competitor moved | direct, indirect, substitute, do nothing and internal build alternatives mapped on buyer-chosen dimensions, every claim attributed |
| [study-audit.md](study-audit.md) | a study, deck or claim is inherited, or a document uses the word "validated" | a ranked defect report against the record each defect lives in, and what the study can still support |
