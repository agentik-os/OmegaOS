# Portfolio Management {OS}: Workflows

Repeatable processes this OS runs. Each workflow is one file, named for what it
produces, and states its trigger, steps, and completion test.

| Workflow | Trigger | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [Onboard a funded position](onboard-a-funded-position.md) | A commitment approved by Capital {OS} has actually funded | The position record: reporting owed, in what form, by whom, by when, plus data rights, contacts, baseline and opening mark |
| [Close a reporting period](close-a-reporting-period.md) | The reporting period ends, or a marking event lands mid period | Every position reported or escalated, every mark with method and evidence, the triage table, and an approved report separating realised from unrealised |
| [Recommend a follow-on](recommend-a-follow-on.md) | A position raises, a right has a deadline, or a stand down decision is due | A follow-on or stand down recommendation with the thesis checkpoint result and current mark, and no amount, handed to Capital {OS} |

The first workflow is the one people skip, and skipping it is what makes the
second one a chase every period. The third one never states a number: it hands
evidence to Capital {OS}, which owns the amount.
