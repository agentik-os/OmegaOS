# Board {OS}: Workflows

Repeatable processes this OS runs. Each workflow is one file, named for what it
produces, and states its trigger, steps, and completion test.

| Workflow | Trigger | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [Prepare and issue a board pack](prepare-and-issue-a-board-pack.md) | the notice deadline, counted backwards from the meeting | a pack issued on time in which every paper names the decision it asks for |
| [Record a board decision](record-a-board-decision.md) | the board reaches a decision, including to defer, decline or delegate | a decision record with attendance, dissent, conflicts and owned actions |
