# Goal & Life Strategy {OS}: Workflows

Repeatable processes this OS runs. Each workflow is one file, named for what it
produces, and states its trigger, steps, and completion test.

| Workflow | Trigger | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [`quarterly-allocation.md`](quarterly-allocation.md) | a quarter closes, or the capacity ceiling from Health & Energy {OS} changes | the next quarter's allocation ledger, the closing review with one correction per divergence, and the ninety-day not-doing list |
| [`annual-life-strategy.md`](annual-life-strategy.md) | the annual reset, or a life change large enough to invalidate the current goal set | the year's life strategy: declared domains, the horizon map, the active goal set with a cost and retirement condition per goal, and the year's not-doing list |

The two compose: the annual pass sets what you are aiming at and what each aim
may cost, the quarterly pass spends against that ceiling and reports where the
spend actually went. Running the quarterly pass with no annual strategy on
record is legal, and it is labelled as a first cycle with no baseline.
