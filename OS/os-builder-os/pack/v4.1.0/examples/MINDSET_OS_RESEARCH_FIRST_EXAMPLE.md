# Example: research-first construction of Mindset {OS}

This is a process example, not the completed Mindset {OS}.

## Input

```text
/os-build Mindset {OS}
```

## Inferred contract

- User: adult seeking clearer interpretation, agency, resilience and intentional action.
- Outcomes: more accurate thinking, better decisions, faster recovery, aligned intention and measurable behavior.
- Non-goals: motivational hype, diagnosis, therapy replacement, guaranteed manifestation.
- Risk: medium because psychological advice can be overgeneralized.

## Book corpus lenses

- foundational cognitive and behavioral models;
- mindset and self-efficacy;
- metacognition and bias;
- acceptance and psychological flexibility;
- motivation, goals and intention;
- resilience and recovery;
- critical works against simplistic positive thinking;
- current evidence-led works.

Every retained book receives a separate deep analysis.

## Non-book expansion

Research verifies claims related to:

- cognitive bias;
- self-efficacy;
- goal intention and implementation;
- psychological flexibility;
- stress and recovery;
- limits of positive thinking;
- when a mindset framing is inappropriate and professional support is needed.

## Synthesis examples

A popular claim such as "belief alone creates outcomes" would likely be separated into:

- beliefs influence attention, persistence and behavior under some conditions;
- beliefs do not override external constraints or guarantee outcomes;
- the OS should convert belief work into observable experiments and evidence;
- high-risk symptoms or severe distress route away from mindset coaching.

## OS compilation

The knowledge could compile into:

```text
EVENT
→ INTERPRETATION
→ EMOTION AND BODY SIGNAL
→ BELIEF OR ASSUMPTION
→ AVAILABLE CHOICES
→ DECISION
→ ACTION
→ RESULT
→ EVIDENCE UPDATE
```

Candidate capabilities:

- narrative detection;
- belief testing;
- intention clarification;
- agency preservation;
- setback recovery;
- evidence logging;
- recurring-pattern detection;
- non-mindset problem routing.

The key difference is that these capabilities are derived from a validated synthesis, not invented from the word "mindset".
