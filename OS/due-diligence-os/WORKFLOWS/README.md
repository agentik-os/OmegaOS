# Due Diligence {OS}: Workflows

Repeatable processes this OS runs. Each workflow is one file, named for what it
produces, and states its trigger, steps, and completion test.

| Workflow | Trigger | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [Scope a diligence plan](scope-a-diligence-plan.md) | an opportunity is qualified and a commitment is contemplated, a data room opens, or requests went out with no plan behind them | a question list where every question names the decision it could change, a stated budget, and the dropped questions with reasons |
| [Verify a management claim](verify-a-management-claim.md) | management asserts something material, or a thesis claim rests only on a seller source | an evidence entry with source, date, confidence and classification, plus a registered finding where the evidence contradicts the claim |
| [Close diligence with conditions](close-diligence-with-conditions.md) | the decision relevant questions are answered, the budget is spent, a stop is called, or completion approaches | the diligence report with its required list of what could not be verified, and the conditions to completion with owners |
