# Trend & Opportunity {OS}: Workflows

Repeatable processes this OS runs. Each workflow is one file, named for what it
produces, and states its trigger, steps, and completion test.

| Workflow | Trigger | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [signal-watchlist.md](signal-watchlist.md) | a domain starts mattering to a decision and nothing is tracking it | a sweepable watch: a named behaviour, independent sources with interest labels, a cadence and a review date |
| [confirmed-movement.md](confirmed-movement.md) | signals have accumulated, or a claimed trend arrives from outside | a movement record with a direction and a rate over a stated window, or a refusal naming what is missing |
| [named-opportunity.md](named-opportunity.md) | movement is confirmed and a specific actor could act on it | an opportunity record: who, what changed, why now, closing condition, expiry date, owner |
| [window-review.md](window-review.md) | a watch reaches its cadence, or an opportunity reaches its review or expiry date | a dated review record per open item, and a retirement record for each window that closed |
