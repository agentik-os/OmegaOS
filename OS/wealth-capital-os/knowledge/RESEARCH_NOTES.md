# Research Notes

## Design synthesis
The OS prioritizes accurate personal books, cash-flow margin, emergency resilience, expensive-debt control, diversified long-term policy and behavioral precommitment. It separates business bookkeeping from personal wealth and treats document ingestion as a controlled accounting workflow.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. official standards, regulation and primary research;
2. official platform documentation and first-party operating material;
3. established academic/practitioner books;
4. case studies and practitioner heuristics;
5. user preference and local experiments.

## Known uncertainty
- Tax and account rules depend on jurisdiction and change over time.
- Future returns, inflation and exchange rates are uncertain.
- Asset valuation may be stale or subjective.
- The user’s risk capacity can change faster than stated risk tolerance.
