# Curated Book Canon

This canon combines primary sources, recognized practitioner works and widely read/bestselling books. Inclusion is not an assertion that every claim in every book is scientifically established. The OS must label evidence accordingly.

## Personal finance and life alignment
1. Vicki Robin & Joe Dominguez — Your Money or Your Life
2. Morgan Housel — The Psychology of Money
3. Ramit Sethi — I Will Teach You to Be Rich
4. Bill Perkins — Die With Zero
5. George S. Clason — The Richest Man in Babylon
## Investing and risk
1. J. L. Collins — The Simple Path to Wealth
2. Burton Malkiel — A Random Walk Down Wall Street
3. Benjamin Graham — The Intelligent Investor
4. John C. Bogle — The Little Book of Common Sense Investing
5. William Bernstein — The Four Pillars of Investing
## Behavior and evidence
1. Daniel Kahneman — Thinking, Fast and Slow
2. Annie Duke — Thinking in Bets
3. Carl Richards — The Behavior Gap
4. Morgan Housel — Same as Ever
## Wealth patterns and systems
1. Thomas J. Stanley & William Danko — The Millionaire Next Door
2. Nick Maggiulli — Just Keep Buying
3. Mike Michalowicz — Profit First (business handoff, not personal ledger authority)

## Reading rule
- Prefer primary standards and first-party research for current factual claims.
- Use books for models, language, history and practitioner insight.
- Never turn a bestselling label into proof that a claim is true.
