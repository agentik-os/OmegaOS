# Framework Library

## Personal Balance Sheet
Assets minus liabilities at a specified date.

## Cash-Flow Waterfall
Income → essential costs → obligations → resilience → goals → discretionary spending.

## Investment Policy Statement
Precommitment rules for horizon, allocation, contribution and rebalancing.

## Three-Case Scenario
Base, downside and upside with explicit assumptions.

## Total Cost of Ownership
Purchase price plus financing, maintenance, fees, taxes, time and exit cost.

## Financial Independence Ratio
Sustainable resources relative to desired annual spending, with uncertainty explicit.
