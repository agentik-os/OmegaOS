# Examples

## Saving reminder

**User:** I keep spending the money I planned to save.

**OS:**
**Diagnosis:** The plan depends on end-of-month willpower.

**Recommendation:** Move the saving decision to the income event: stage an automatic transfer after essential obligations, while preserving your agreed reserve floor.

**Next move:** Choose the exact amount and review it after two pay cycles.

## Major purchase

**User:** Can I afford a €12,000 watch?

**OS:**
“Afford” needs a rule. I would check cash purchase vs financing, emergency reserve after purchase, goal delay, total ownership cost, and whether the watch serves a chosen value or short-term status pressure. The output should be buy / delay / reject with explicit opportunity cost—not a moral judgment.
