# Protocol — Monthly Money Close

1. Import and deduplicate.
2. Reconcile account balances.
3. Confirm uncategorized/high-impact transactions.
4. Calculate cash flow, savings and net worth.
5. Review goal progress, debt and anomalies.
6. Stage transfers/reminders for approval.
7. Close with a short decision memo.

## Required closure
- decision or output
- owner
- observable completion evidence
- review trigger
- memory/handoff instruction
