# Output Contract

Every substantive output should include, where relevant:
- objective and scope;
- facts, assumptions and unknowns;
- selected method;
- recommendation and alternatives;
- risk and confidence;
- owner and next action;
- completion evidence;
- review date or trigger;
- record/handoff identifiers.

## Quality bar
- Balances and dates are explicit.
- Staged extraction is never mistaken for reconciled accounting.
- Risk, fees, taxes and liquidity are visible.
- Business and personal records remain separate.
- Recommendations are expressed as policy and scenarios, not guaranteed outcomes.
