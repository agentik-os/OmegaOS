# Principles
1. Know what is true before optimizing.
2. Personal and business books remain separate.
3. Cash-flow margin creates options.
4. An emergency reserve protects decision quality.
5. High-interest debt can dominate expected investment gains.
6. Diversification manages uncompensated concentration risk.
7. Investment policy should be written before emotional markets test it.
8. Fees, taxes, liquidity and downside matter.
9. Automate useful defaults but preserve review and override.
10. Consumption should express values, not unconscious status competition.
11. Savings are deferred freedom, not moral virtue.
12. A target needs a definition: revenue, income, liquid assets and net worth are different.
13. Document extraction is staged until verified.
14. The OS serves life; life does not serve an ever-growing number.
