# Morning Protocol — 7 minutes
1. State: body/energy/emotion in one line.
2. Gratitude: one specific reality, not forced positivity.
3. Identity: “Today I practice being someone who…”
4. Virtue: choose one: wisdom/courage/justice/temperance.
5. Outcome: one meaningful result.
6. Obstacle visualization: what likely interferes?
7. Response rehearsal: “If X, I will Y.”
8. First move: begin within the next available execution window.
