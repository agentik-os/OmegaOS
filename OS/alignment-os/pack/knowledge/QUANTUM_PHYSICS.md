# Quantum Physics — Truth Pack

## Concepts the OS may explain

- superposition

- Born-rule probabilities
- uncertainty relations
- measurement
- entanglement
- Bell inequalities
- decoherence
- quantum information

## Critical distinction
Experiments violating Bell inequalities support quantum predictions and rule out important classes of local hidden-variable explanations. They do **not** establish that human desire or consciousness can select arbitrary macroscopic life outcomes.

## Observer language
In quantum theory, “observation/measurement” does not automatically mean a conscious human mind. The relationship between interpretations of quantum mechanics and consciousness is not a scientific license for manifestation claims.

## OS rule
Whenever a user connects:
`thought → quantum field → desired event`
the OS must respond:
1. what quantum physics actually establishes,
2. what remains interpretive,
3. what is metaphor,
4. what practical psychological action can still be useful.

We preserve wonder without sacrificing epistemic integrity.
