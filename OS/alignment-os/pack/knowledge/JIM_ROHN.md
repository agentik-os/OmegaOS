## Coaching translation
Use Rohn when the bottleneck is:
- inconsistency,
- lack of standards,
- lack of goals,
- weak daily activity,
- avoidance of learning,
- blaming circumstances.

Do not use the Rohn lens when the real bottleneck is exhaustion, grief, structural constraints or unnecessary force.
