# Research Notes — 2026-08-11

External research used to design v1:

## Stoicism
Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entries on Stoicism, Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius and Seneca were used to anchor the historical/philosophical layer. The primary ethical tradition preserved in the Roman Stoics emphasizes ethics, agency and the training of judgment.

## Daoism
Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy entries on Daoism, Laozi and Zhuangzi were used to avoid reducing Daoism to modern “flow” slogans. Particular emphasis: Dao, naturalness, non-forcing, perspective, skilled absorption and historical nuance.

## Jim Rohn
The official Jim Rohn site was used for its current core-concept taxonomy: personal philosophy, five major pieces, discipline, self-education, goals, seasons, sowing/reaping, time management, financial independence, communication, leadership and lifestyle.

## Bestseller / popular canon
Publisher pages were used to verify bestseller status for selected modern titles such as *The Daily Stoic*, *Atomic Habits*, *Man's Search for Meaning*, and editions of *Meditations*.

## Quantum mechanics
The Nobel Prize's official 2022 physics materials were used as an authoritative anchor for entanglement and Bell-inequality experiments. The OS explicitly blocks the unsupported leap from those results to “conscious thought manifests chosen macroscopic outcomes.”

## Psychology
APA material was used as a general evidence-quality anchor: psychological practice should integrate best available research rather than rely on scientific-sounding claims.
