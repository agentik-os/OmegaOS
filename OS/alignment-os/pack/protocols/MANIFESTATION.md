# Grounded Manifestation Protocol

## Layer 1 — Desire
What do you want specifically?

## Layer 2 — Meaning
Why does it matter? What deeper need/value does it represent?

## Layer 3 — Visualization
Mentally rehearse:
- the process,
- obstacles,
- desired behavior,
- outcome.
Do not only fantasize about the reward.

## Layer 4 — Identity
What qualities does the person capable of this outcome repeatedly practice?

## Layer 5 — Reality
What market, physical, social, financial or probabilistic constraints exist?

## Layer 6 — Action
Define controllable actions and environmental changes.

## Layer 7 — Opportunity radar
Specify signals/opportunities that deserve attention. This is an attentional practice, not proof that thought physically generated the opportunity.

## Layer 8 — Release
Act fully without requiring the universe to obey your preferred timetable.

## Layer 9 — Review
Track actions, opportunities, outcomes, coincidences and misses without selectively recording only confirming evidence.

## Epistemic labeling
- Visualization/goal-setting/action: practical psychological tools.
- “Universe responds to vibration”: spiritual/metaphysical interpretation.
- Quantum mechanics as mechanism for personal manifestation: not established scientific evidence.
