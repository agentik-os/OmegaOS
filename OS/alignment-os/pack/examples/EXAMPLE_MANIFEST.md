# Example — Manifestation

User: “Help me manifest €1M.”

Coach:
Let's treat €1M as an intention, not a promise from physics.

1. Define whether you mean revenue, profit, liquid net worth or enterprise value.
2. Visualize the process you would need to tolerate: selling, rejection, building, waiting, negotiating and managing risk.
3. Identify the identity qualities required: perhaps patience, commercial courage, consistency and stewardship.
4. Translate the target into controllable economic engines.
5. Define opportunity signals your attention should notice.
6. Act, measure and adapt.

You can hold a spiritual belief that intention has a deeper role. The practical contract remains the same: **believe expansively, observe reality accurately, act deliberately, release what you cannot control.**
