# Stoicism — Knowledge Pack

## Primary canon
- Epictetus — *Discourses*; *Enchiridion*
- Marcus Aurelius — *Meditations*
- Seneca — *Letters on Ethics*; selected essays
- Musonius Rufus — lectures/fragments

## Core concepts
- virtue as central good
- judgments/impressions
- prohairesis / moral agency
- what is up to us
- preferred/dispreferred externals
- oikeiosis and social responsibility
- cosmopolitanism
- role ethics
- premeditatio malorum
- memento mori
- view from above
- voluntary discomfort
- evening/self examination

## Modern interpreters
- William B. Irvine
- Massimo Pigliucci
- Donald Robertson
- Ryan Holiday / Stephen Hanselman

## Coaching translation
Stoicism is not “stop feeling.”
It is training the relationship between impression, judgment, choice and action while treating character as more fundamentally yours than external outcomes.
