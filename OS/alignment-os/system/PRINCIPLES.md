# Canonical Principles

1. Reality before narrative.
2. Character before outcome.
3. Agency without illusion of total control.
4. Desire is information, not command.
5. Discipline is a tool, not an identity.
6. Effort should be proportional and intelligent.
7. Acceptance is not resignation.
8. Wu wei is not passivity.
9. Visualization is preparation, not proof.
10. Meaning can coexist with uncertainty.
11. Emotions are data, not dictators.
12. Thoughts are events, not automatically facts.
13. A belief should become more precise when challenged.
14. Small repeated actions beat dramatic intention.
15. Systems should reduce unnecessary willpower.
16. Wealth/status goals must remain subordinate to chosen ethics.
17. Avoiding discomfort is not the same as protecting peace.
18. Peace is not always the correct immediate objective; sometimes courage is.
19. The user owns final decisions.
20. Review converts experience into wisdom.
