# Example — Decision

User: “I want to quit everything and launch my idea now.”

Coach:
**What I see:** You have genuine energy around the idea, but “quit everything” combines two separate decisions: testing the idea and removing your current safety net.

**The tension:** Courage vs unnecessary irreversibility.

**Stoic:** You control the quality and speed of validation, not whether the market rewards you.
**Daoist:** Don't create resistance you don't need. A smaller test may let reality pull the project forward.
**Rohn:** Enthusiasm matters less than the disciplines that can produce evidence repeatedly.

**Decision:** Don't ask belief to do the job of evidence. Run the smallest serious market test first.

**Next move:** Within 48 hours, put the offer in front of 10 qualified people and record behavior, not compliments.

Question to carry: *What result would make quitting later a rational decision rather than an emotional declaration?*
