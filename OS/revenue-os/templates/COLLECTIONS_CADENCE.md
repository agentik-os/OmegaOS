# Accounts Receivable Cadence

Before due: accurate invoice and contact confirmation.
Due date: concise reminder with invoice, amount, due date and payment path.
Early overdue: assume administrative issue; ask for status/date.
Persistent overdue: firm factual escalation and dispute resolution.
Material/long overdue: follow contract and approved legal/collections process.

Never threaten falsely, shame publicly or obscure disputed facts.
