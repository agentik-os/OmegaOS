- offer

RELATIONSHIPS
- business_entity HAS customers/offers/invoices/expenses
- customer HAS contacts/opportunities/contracts/invoices/activities
- offer TARGETS customer and PRICES opportunity
- opportunity MAY BECOME contract
- contract AUTHORIZES invoice
- invoice MATCHED_BY payment
- financial_document SUPPORTS contract/invoice/expense/payment
- forecast USES opportunities/invoices/expenses
- reserve_policy GOVERNS business cash
- Delivery OS PROVIDES value evidence

GLOBAL FIELDS
- id
- created_at
- updated_at
- source
- confidence
- owner / subject where applicable
- consent / access policy where sensitive
