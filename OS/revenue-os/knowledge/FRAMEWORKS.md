# Framework Library

## Offer Equation
Desired outcome and perceived likelihood relative to time delay and effort/sacrifice.

## Obviously Awesome Positioning
Best-fit customer, alternatives, unique attributes, value and category.

## Value-Based Pricing
Price around differentiated customer value and willingness to pay, constrained by economics and fairness.

## SPIN / Gap / Challenger
Situation/problem/implication/need-payoff; current-to-future gap; commercial insight when appropriate.

## MEDDICC-Inspired Qualification
Metrics, buyer, criteria, process, pain, champion and competition—adapted, not ritualized.

## Pipeline Stage Exit Criteria
Evidence-based movement rather than rep opinion.

## Three-Level Forecast
Commit, best case and pipeline with range assumptions.

## Invoice-to-Cash
Contract → invoice → delivery evidence → due → collection → reconciliation.

## AR Aging
Current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ or policy-defined buckets.

## Cash vs Accrual
Cash movement differs from economic recognition.

## Revenue Waterfall
Opening contract/bookings/billings/deferred/recognized/collected movements as applicable.

## Unit Economics
Revenue minus variable/direct cost, acquisition and service capacity.

## Customer 360
Commercial + delivery + billing + realized-value record with access controls.
