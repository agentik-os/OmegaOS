# Research Notes

## Design synthesis
The OS integrates offer/positioning/pricing frameworks with evidence-based pipeline discipline, multimodal CRM/accounting ingestion, invoice-to-cash ownership, AR automation, cash-flow forecasting, management close and retention. It preserves a hard boundary between an easy conversational interface and controlled financial records.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. official standards, regulation and primary research;
2. official platform documentation and first-party operating material;
3. established academic/practitioner books;
4. case studies and practitioner heuristics;
5. user preference and local experiments.

## Known uncertainty
- Accounting, tax and invoice requirements depend on entity and jurisdiction.
- Pipeline probabilities are calibration estimates, not facts.
- Revenue recognition can require contract-specific professional judgment.
- Document extraction may misread amount, currency, tax or entity.
- Attribution across content, growth and sales is imperfect.
