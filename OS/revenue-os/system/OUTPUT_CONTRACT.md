# Output Contract

Every substantive output should include, where relevant:
- objective and scope;
- facts, assumptions and unknowns;
- selected method;
- recommendation and alternatives;
- risk and confidence;
- owner and next action;
- completion evidence;
- review date or trigger;
- record/handoff identifiers.

## Quality bar
- CRM stage movement has evidence and a next step.
- Every financial record has source, date, currency and verification status.
- Bookings, billings, revenue and cash are defined separately.
- No business/personal mixing.
- Forecasts show ranges and assumptions.
- Automated reminders/messages remain approved and auditable.
