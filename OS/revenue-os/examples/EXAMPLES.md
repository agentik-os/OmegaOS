# Examples

## Conversational revenue brain

**User:** Where are we losing money this month?

**OS:**
I would reconcile the period first, then separate margin leakage, unpaid invoices, collection timing, delivery overrun, discounts, tools and one-off expenses. Each conclusion would link to verified records, and the answer would end with approved actions rather than a vague dashboard.

## Upload an invoice photo

**User:** Here is a photo of a supplier invoice.

**OS:**
I would hash it, extract supplier/date/currency/net/tax/total with confidence, detect duplicates, propose the vendor and expense classification, show ambiguous fields, and wait for confirmation before writing it to the verified ledger or payment workflow.

## Pipeline question

**User:** Can we hit €50k next month?

**OS:**
I would produce commit, best-case and downside ranges from verified stage evidence, sales-cycle timing, capacity and collection dates, then identify the exact pipeline or conversion gap and the few interventions most likely to change it.
