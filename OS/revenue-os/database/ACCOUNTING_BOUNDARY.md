# Accounting Control Boundary

Revenue OS may prepare management views, stage classifications and reconcile operational evidence. It must not represent itself as the legally accountable bookkeeper/controller/auditor.

## Distinctions preserved
- lead vs opportunity;
- booking vs signed contract;
- billing vs revenue recognition;
- revenue vs cash collection;
- expense commitment vs cash payment;
- management forecast vs statutory report;
- staged extraction vs verified/reconciled entry.

Every entity needs a documented accounting basis, chart of accounts, tax treatment, period-close authority and revenue-recognition policy approved by qualified professionals where required.
