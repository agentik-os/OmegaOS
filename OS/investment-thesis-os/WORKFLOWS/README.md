# Investment Thesis {OS}: Workflows

Repeatable processes this OS runs. Each workflow is one file, named for what it
produces, and states its trigger, steps, and completion test.

| Workflow | Trigger | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| [Write a thesis before a commitment](write-a-thesis-before-a-commitment.md) | an opportunity is qualified and a commitment is contemplated, or Capital {OS} is asked for an allocation with no thesis reference | a timestamped thesis with falsifiable claims, kill criteria and a checkpoint date, stored before money moves |
| [Run a thesis checkpoint](run-a-thesis-checkpoint.md) | a checkpoint date or milestone arrives, a follow on is considered, or a red flag or impairment lands | a claim by claim verdict against dated evidence, a kill criteria result, and a stored checkpoint record |
| [Retire a thesis and update the hit rate](retire-a-thesis-and-update-the-hit-rate.md) | a position is exited, written off, never entered, or superseded | a retirement verdict, a wrong versus unlucky call with its basis, an updated hit rate and a pattern entry |
