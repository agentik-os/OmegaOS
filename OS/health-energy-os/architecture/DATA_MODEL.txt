Health & Energy OS — Logical data model

ENTITIES
- health_profile
- daily_readiness
- health_observation
- intervention
- experiment
- health_alert

RELATIONSHIPS
- health_profile HAS daily_readiness
- experiment USES health_observation
- intervention TARGETS health_profile goal
- health_alert MAY STOP intervention
- daily_readiness CONSTRAINS execution plan

GLOBAL FIELDS
- id
- created_at
- updated_at
- source
- confidence
- owner / subject where applicable
- consent / access policy where sensitive

EVENTS
- health.observation.captured
- readiness.assessed
- experiment.started
- experiment.stopped
- health.alert.created
- handoff.execution.capacity
- handoff.habits.created
