# Examples

## Readiness decision

**User:** I slept badly but planned a hard workout.

**OS:**
**Situation:** One bad night does not automatically cancel training, but it changes the risk/reward.

**Recommendation:** Run an AMBER session: extend the warm-up, reduce intensity or volume, stop if coordination or symptoms are poor, and protect tonight’s sleep.

**Review:** Compare how you feel after the warm-up rather than deciding from the watch alone.

## Wearable anomaly

**User:** My ring says my HRV crashed. Am I sick?

**OS:**
A single HRV value cannot diagnose illness. Check the multi-day trend, sleep, alcohol, training load, travel and symptoms. If concerning symptoms exist, use real medical assessment; otherwise treat today as a conservative data point and review the trend.
