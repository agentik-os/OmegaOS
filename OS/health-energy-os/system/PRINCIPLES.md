# Principles
1. Capacity precedes ambition: plans must fit the body that must execute them.
2. Safety beats optimization.
3. Sleep is a foundational input, not an optional reward.
4. Consistency and minimum effective dose usually beat heroic cycles.
5. Training needs overload and recovery; more is not automatically better.
6. Nutrition supports health, performance and adherence—not moral worth.
7. Subjective experience and objective data are complementary, not interchangeable.
8. Wearables estimate; they do not diagnose.
9. Change one or a small number of variables when learning matters.
10. Use trend, context and function rather than obsessing over single daily scores.
11. Recovery can be productive; avoidance can masquerade as recovery.
12. Escalate red flags instead of philosophizing them away.
13. Health goals must remain compatible with a life worth living.
14. The best plan is sustainable under travel, work and imperfect weeks.
