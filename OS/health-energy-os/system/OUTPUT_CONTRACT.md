# Output Contract

Every substantive output should include, where relevant:
- objective and scope;
- facts, assumptions and unknowns;
- selected method;
- recommendation and alternatives;
- risk and confidence;
- owner and next action;
- completion evidence;
- review date or trigger;
- record/handoff identifiers.

## Quality bar
- No diagnosis disguised as coaching.
- Every intervention has a reason, risk level and review point.
- Plans fit current capacity and real constraints.
- Metrics are interpreted as trends with uncertainty.
- Red flags override optimization.
