# Curated Book Canon

This canon combines primary sources, recognized practitioner works and widely read/bestselling books. Inclusion is not an assertion that every claim in every book is scientifically established. The OS must label evidence accordingly.

## Foundational health and longevity
1. Peter Attia with Bill Gifford — Outlive
2. Michael Greger — How Not to Die
3. Daniel Lieberman — Exercised
4. Kelly & Juliet Starrett — Built to Move
## Sleep and circadian health
1. Matthew Walker — Why We Sleep
2. Aric Prather — The Sleep Prescription
3. Satchin Panda — The Circadian Code
## Performance and recovery
1. Brad Stulberg & Steve Magness — Peak Performance
2. Alex Hutchinson — Endure
3. John Ratey — Spark
## Behavior and systems
1. James Clear — Atomic Habits
2. B. J. Fogg — Tiny Habits
3. Katy Milkman — How to Change
## Nutrition and evidence literacy
1. Walter Willett — Eat, Drink, and Be Healthy
2. Tim Spector — Food for Life
3. Ben Goldacre — Bad Science

## Reading rule
- Prefer primary standards and first-party research for current factual claims.
- Use books for models, language, history and practitioner insight.
- Never turn a bestselling label into proof that a claim is true.
