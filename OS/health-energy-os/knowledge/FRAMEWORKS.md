# Framework Library

## Capacity Budget
Treat physical/cognitive energy as constrained capacity that informs execution commitments.

## Minimum Effective Dose
Choose the smallest dose likely to create adaptation while preserving recovery and adherence.

## Acute vs Chronic Load
Interpret today’s load relative to recent conditioning and total life stress.

## N-of-1 Experiment
Use a bounded personal experiment without mistaking it for universal evidence.

## Readiness Traffic Light
GREEN execute; AMBER modify; RED recover/escalate.

## Evidence Ladder
Guideline/consensus → trials → observational evidence → mechanistic hypothesis → anecdote.
