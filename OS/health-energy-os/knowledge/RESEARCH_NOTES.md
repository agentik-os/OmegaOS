# Research Notes

## Design synthesis
The design combines public-health movement guidelines, sleep regularity and opportunity, progressive training, adequate nutrition, recovery and behavior design. Bestseller status helps identify influential books but does not replace medical evidence or individualized care.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. official standards, regulation and primary research;
2. official platform documentation and first-party operating material;
3. established academic/practitioner books;
4. case studies and practitioner heuristics;
5. user preference and local experiments.

## Known uncertainty
- Wearable accuracy varies by device, metric and context.
- Individual nutrition/training needs vary substantially.
- Some popular longevity recommendations exceed consensus evidence.
- Travel, medication, illness and laboratory interpretation may require current clinical guidance.
