# Delivery & Customer Success {OS}

Fulfil the promise, drive adoption, earn the renewal. Everything after the
signature, up to and including the recommendation to renew.

## When to use this

Reach for Delivery & Customer Success {OS} once a commitment is signed: taking
the handoff from Sales {OS}, onboarding a new customer, agreeing what success
means in the customer's own words, running delivery and its communication,
handling a scope change or an escalation, driving adoption when delivery landed
but usage did not, proving realised value at a review, recommending a renewal or
an expansion, asking for a case study, or offboarding well.

Near neighbours it is confused with:

- **Sales {OS}** owns the pipeline and the close. This OS starts where that
  ends, and it inherits every promise made along the way.
- **Offer {OS}** owns what is sold and its scope boundary. This OS delivers
  against that boundary and escalates anything outside it rather than absorbing
  it.
- **Revenue {OS}** owns the contract, the invoice and the renewal DECISION. This
  OS owns fulfilment, adoption and the renewal SIGNALS.
- **Quality & Evaluation {OS}** owns whether the work meets standard. This OS
  owns whether the customer accepted it, which is a different event.
- **Storyteller {OS}** and **Content {OS}** own how a customer story is told.
  This OS owns the consent and the evidence behind it.
- **Network {OS}** owns the human relationship independent of the engagement. A
  customer contact can exist in both, as two records that do not merge.

The discriminating question: **has the customer signed, and has payment
reconciled?** Before that gate, it is Sales {OS} and Revenue {OS}. After it, it
is this OS.

## Capabilities

- Run the sales to delivery handoff and itemise every promise made in the sale.
- Check each promise against the Offer {OS} scope boundary and escalate the gaps.
- Enforce the post-payment gate: contract signed, payment reconciled, handoff accepted.
- Build an onboarding plan with a first value date the customer can feel.
- Write a success plan carrying the customer's own measures, agreed in writing.
- Produce status updates that surface risk with options before the deadline.
- Process a scope change: price it or refuse it, and record the decision.
- Build an escalation plan when health, dates or trust are at risk.
- Record acceptance from customer evidence, never from an internal status field.
- Design adoption interventions with measurable targets.
- Compile value proof that attributes honestly and names what it cannot attribute.
- Prepare a business review that would survive the customer reading it line by line.
- Produce a renewal recommendation with its signals, for Revenue {OS} to decide on.
- Obtain scoped, written case study consent and show the customer first.
- Offboard preserving dignity, data control and the learning.

## Procedure

1. Verify the post-payment gate: `contract.signed`, then `payment.reconciled`,
   then `handoff.accepted`. If any link is missing, name it and stop. Work does
   not start before the gate clears.
2. Take the handoff from Sales {OS} and itemise every promise made in the sale
   into the promise register, each with its source.
3. Check every promise against the Offer {OS} scope boundary. In scope becomes a
   delivery obligation. Outside scope becomes an escalation before it is work.
4. Run discovery with the customer and capture their definition of success in
   their words, with measures they agree to in writing.
5. Build the success plan and the delivery plan against the promise register.
6. Deliver, and communicate status with risk stated early and with options.
7. Route every scope change to a price or a refusal. Never absorb one silently.
8. Close each deliverable with acceptance evidence produced by the customer.
9. Measure adoption. Where usage did not follow delivery, intervene against a
   measurable target rather than a guess.
10. Compile value proof, attributing what is attributable and stating what is not.
11. Produce the renewal recommendation with its signals and send it to Revenue
    {OS}. Never commit a term here.
12. Present every customer-facing text for explicit approval on the exact
    wording before it is sent.

## Handoffs

- **Revenue {OS}** receives the acceptance record, the adoption signal and the
  renewal recommendation. It expects acceptance to be evidenced by a customer
  act, and it owns the renewal decision that follows.
- **Growth {OS}** receives the acceptance record and adoption signal as
  retention and adoption evidence. It expects cohorts and signals defined
  consistently across periods.
- **Storyteller {OS}** receives consented case study material: the outcome, the
  evidence and the scope of the consent. It expects consent to be written,
  scoped per quote and figure, and revocable.
- **Content {OS}** receives the same consented material for packaging, and must
  obey the same consent scope.
- **Sales {OS}** hands this OS the signed commitment and every promise made in
  the sale; it expects a gap it created to be raised, not silently absorbed.
- **Offer {OS}** hands this OS the scope boundary and guarantee, and receives
  back what fulfilment actually costs to deliver.
- **Quality & Evaluation {OS}** hands this OS acceptance evidence for the work
  itself, which is an input to customer acceptance and not a substitute for it.
